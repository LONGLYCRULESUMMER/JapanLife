# Tax Guide for Freelancers and Self-Employed Foreigners in Japan

## Filing Obligation

If you are self-employed, freelancing, or have business income (事業所得) in Japan, you MUST file a tax return (確定申告) annually. There is no year-end adjustment for self-employed individuals.

## Blue Return vs White Return

### Blue Return (青色申告)
- Must register in advance with the tax office (within 2 months of starting business)
- Special deductions: ¥100,000 (simple bookkeeping) or ¥550,000/¥650,000 (double-entry bookkeeping with e-Tax)
- Can carry forward losses for 3 years
- Can deduct family member salaries as business expenses
- Requires maintaining proper accounting records

### White Return (白色申告)
- No advance registration needed
- No special deduction
- Simpler record-keeping requirements
- Suitable for very small-scale freelancing

## Deductible Business Expenses (経費)

Common deductible expenses for freelancers:
- Home office expenses (proportional to business use, e.g., 30% of rent)
- Equipment: computer, phone, office supplies
- Communication: internet, phone bills (business portion)
- Transportation: business travel, commuting to client sites
- Professional development: books, courses, certifications
- Software and subscriptions
- Business insurance
- Depreciation on expensive equipment (over ¥100,000)

## Estimated Tax Payments (予定納税)

If your previous year's tax was ¥150,000 or more, you must make estimated payments:
- First installment: by July 31
- Second installment: by November 30
- Each installment = 1/3 of previous year's tax

## Consumption Tax (消費税)

- Businesses with sales over ¥10,000,000 must register for consumption tax
- Invoice system (インボイス制度) implemented October 2023
- Registration required to issue qualified invoices
- Consider registering even if below threshold if your clients need invoices

## Social Insurance for Self-Employed

- Must enroll in National Health Insurance (国民健康保険) at ward office
- Must enroll in National Pension (国民年金) — Category 1
- No unemployment insurance for self-employed
- Premiums are fully tax-deductible

## Tips for Foreign Freelancers

1. Register as a sole proprietor (個人事業の開業届) at tax office within 1 month of starting
2. Apply for Blue Return status immediately for maximum deductions
3. Use accounting software: freee, MoneyForward, Yayoi (some have English interfaces)
4. Keep ALL receipts — digital copies acceptable
5. Separate business and personal bank accounts
6. Consider hiring a tax accountant (税理士) for the first year
