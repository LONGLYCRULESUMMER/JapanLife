# Employment Income and Withholding

## How withholding works

Most employees in Japan have national income tax withheld from each paycheck by their employer. The employer estimates tax using salary amount, submitted dependent information, and standard payroll tables, then pays the withheld amount to the tax office. This withholding is a prepayment system; it is not always the final tax for the year.

## Year-end adjustment

Year-end adjustment (年末調整) is the employer's annual reconciliation of salary income tax. Employees submit forms for dependents, insurance premiums, spouse information, and similar items. The employer compares the year's withholding with the final salary-tax calculation and refunds or collects the difference through payroll.

## When filing is still needed

A final tax return may still be useful or required when year-end adjustment does not cover the situation. Common examples include income from multiple employers, side income, medical expense deductions, first-year housing loan deduction, foreign income, or leaving Japan before year-end settlement is complete.

## Tips for Foreign Residents

Keep every Certificate of Income and Tax Withheld (源泉徴収票). Check that your name, address, My Number handling, dependents, and social-insurance deductions match your records. If you have overseas income, dependents abroad, or treaty questions, confirm the treatment early rather than waiting for filing season.
