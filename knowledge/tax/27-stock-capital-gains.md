# Stock Capital Gains

## Separate taxation

Gains from listed shares and certain securities are generally handled under a separate taxation framework rather than combined with ordinary salary income. Dividends and capital gains can have different reporting choices, so check how your account and products are treated.

## Withholding accounts

Japanese brokers may offer specified accounts, including accounts with withholding. In a withholding account, the broker calculates and withholds tax on eligible trades, which can reduce filing work. Filing may still be useful for losses, deductions, or combining accounts.

## Foreign broker issues

Foreign brokers usually do not prepare Japanese tax forms or withhold Japanese tax. You may need to calculate gains, dividends, foreign tax, exchange rates, and supporting records yourself. Keep annual statements and transaction-level data.

## Loss carryforward

Losses from listed shares may be carried forward or offset under specific filing rules when a final tax return is submitted. If you want to preserve loss treatment, check the filing requirements even in a year with no tax due.
