# Overseas Dependent Documents

## Relationship documents

When claiming deductions for relatives living outside Japan, documents may be needed to show the family relationship. Examples can include government-issued family registers, birth certificates, marriage certificates, or equivalent documents from the relevant country.

## Remittance documents

Remittance documents show that you provided financial support to the overseas relative. Bank transfer records, remittance service statements, or card payment records may be used when they clearly connect the sender, recipient, date, and amount.

## Translation needs

Documents not written in Japanese may need a Japanese translation. The translation should make names, dates, relationship terms, and issuing authorities clear. Keep both the original document and the translation together.

## Tips

Collect documents before year-end adjustment or filing season. Names can appear in different orders or spellings across passports, bank records, and civil documents, so keep explanatory notes where needed. Do not rely on cash hand-carry support if it leaves no clear record.
