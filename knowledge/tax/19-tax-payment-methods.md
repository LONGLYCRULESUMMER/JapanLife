# How to Pay National Tax

## Bank and convenience store payment

National tax can often be paid through financial institutions using payment slips. Some payments may also be available at convenience stores when the required barcode or payment information is issued. Confirm the available method for the specific tax, amount, and due date.

## e-Tax and direct payment

e-Tax supports electronic filing and payment options. Direct payment and internet banking methods can reduce paper handling, but they may require advance setup with a bank account and e-Tax credentials. Register early if you plan to use electronic payment near a deadline.

## Credit card payment

Credit card payment may be available through the designated national tax payment site. Card payments can involve service fees and may not be treated as paid until the procedure is completed correctly. Check card limits and confirmation screens before relying on this method.

## Keep proof of payment

Save receipts, confirmation numbers, bank records, and e-Tax messages. Proof of payment can be important when applying for certificates, responding to reminders, or reconciling resident-tax and national-tax records later.
