# Correcting a Tax Return

## Correction before deadline

If you discover an error before the filing deadline, you can usually submit a corrected return for the same year. The latest valid filing before the deadline is used for processing. Keep a note of what changed and why.

## Amended return

After the deadline, an amended return is generally used when the correction increases tax due or reduces an overclaimed refund. Additional tax and delinquent tax may apply depending on timing. Filing voluntarily soon after discovering the error is usually safer than waiting.

## Claim for correction

A claim for correction is generally used when a filed return overstated tax or understated a refund. It asks the tax office to review and correct the amount. Supporting documents are important because the claim explains why the original filing should be changed.

## Tips

Do not edit only your personal copy and assume the tax office knows. Save the original return, corrected documents, calculations, and submission receipts. If the error affects resident tax, health insurance, or immigration certificates, check whether downstream records will update automatically.
