# Pension Lump-Sum Tax Reclaim

## Lump-sum withdrawal

People who leave Japan after paying into the Japanese pension system may be eligible for a lump-sum withdrawal payment. The pension claim is handled through the Japan Pension Service after leaving Japan and depends on contribution history and other conditions.

## Withholding tax

A tax amount may be withheld from the lump-sum withdrawal payment. This withholding is separate from the pension application itself. Depending on the facts, part of the withheld tax may be reclaimable through Japanese income-tax procedures.

## Tax representative

A person outside Japan generally cannot complete all Japanese tax procedures alone from abroad. Appointing a tax representative before or after departure allows someone in Japan to file the reclaim paperwork and receive notices. Choose someone reliable and keep copies of the appointment documents.

## Timeline

The pension payment and tax reclaim are separate steps, so the full timeline can take months. Keep your pension book or number, departure records, bank details, tax representative documents, and copies of all submissions until both processes are complete.
