# Tax Certificates

## Income certificate

An income certificate (課税証明書 or 所得証明書, depending on municipality wording) shows reported income and resident-tax assessment information for a tax year. It is often used to prove income for housing, childcare, visa, or loan procedures.

## Tax payment certificate

A tax payment certificate (納税証明書) shows whether assessed local taxes have been paid. Immigration and permanent residence procedures often focus on payment history, not only income amount, so unpaid or late taxes can matter.

## Where to request

Municipal tax certificates are usually requested from the city or ward office that assessed the resident tax for the relevant year. National tax certificates are handled separately by the tax office. Many municipalities support convenience-store issuance with My Number Card, but availability varies.

## Visa and PR use

For visa renewal or permanent residence, check the exact certificate names and years requested by Immigration Services. Request certificates early if you recently moved, because the municipality that issues the certificate may not be your current city office.
