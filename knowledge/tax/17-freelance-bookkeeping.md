# Bookkeeping for Freelancers

## Income and expenses

Freelancers should track income when it is earned or received according to their accounting method, and expenses when they relate to business activity. Separate business and personal transactions where possible. Record client name, invoice number, date, amount, consumption tax treatment, and payment status.

## Receipts

Receipts, invoices, card statements, bank records, and contracts support the numbers on the return. A receipt alone is easier to understand when it includes a note explaining the business purpose. Digital copies can be useful, but keep them organized and readable.

## Home office and utilities

Home office, internet, phone, and utilities may include both personal and business use. Use a reasonable allocation method, such as floor space or actual business usage, and apply it consistently. Keep notes showing how the business portion was calculated.

## Retention period

Tax records are expected to be retained for multiple years after filing. Retention rules vary by document type and filing status, so check current NTA guidance before discarding anything. A simple folder structure by tax year makes later questions much easier to answer.
