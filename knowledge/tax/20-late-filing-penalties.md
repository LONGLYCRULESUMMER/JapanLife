# Late Filing and Penalties

## Late filing surcharge

When a required final tax return is filed after the due date, a late filing surcharge may apply. The amount depends on facts such as timing, whether the tax office contacted you first, and the unpaid tax. Filing voluntarily before contact is generally better than waiting.

## Delinquent tax

Delinquent tax is interest-like tax on late payment. It can apply even when the return is filed but the tax is paid after the deadline. Paying as soon as possible helps reduce the period used in the calculation.

## Voluntary correction

If you find an omission, review whether a correction, amended return, or claim for correction is appropriate. The best procedure depends on whether the original return was filed, whether tax increases or decreases, and whether the filing deadline has passed.

## Prevention tips

Put filing dates on a calendar, keep documents throughout the year, and file early when you expect a refund or need missing documents. If you cannot complete everything, ask the tax office or a tax accountant about the safest next step before the deadline.
