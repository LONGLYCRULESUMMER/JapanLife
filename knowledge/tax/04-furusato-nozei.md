# Furusato Nozei (ふるさと納税) Guide for Foreigners in Japan

## What is Furusato Nozei?

Furusato Nozei (hometown tax donation) is a system that allows you to donate money to municipalities across Japan in exchange for:
1. Local specialty gifts (return gifts / 返礼品) worth up to 30% of your donation
2. Tax deductions on your income tax and resident tax

Effectively, you pay ¥2,000 out of pocket and receive local products in return while redirecting your taxes to municipalities of your choice.

## Eligibility

- Anyone who pays resident tax in Japan can participate
- This includes foreigners on work visas, spouse visas, etc.
- You must have a Japanese address registered with your municipality
- Your My Number is required for the donation

## How Much Can You Donate?

Your deductible limit depends on your income and family situation:

| Annual Income | Single | Married (1 child) |
|---|---|---|
| ¥3,000,000 | ~¥28,000 | ~¥19,000 |
| ¥4,000,000 | ~¥42,000 | ~¥33,000 |
| ¥5,000,000 | ~¥61,000 | ~¥49,000 |
| ¥6,000,000 | ~¥77,000 | ~¥69,000 |
| ¥7,000,000 | ~¥108,000 | ~¥86,000 |
| ¥8,000,000 | ~¥129,000 | ~¥120,000 |
| ¥10,000,000 | ~¥176,000 | ~¥166,000 |

Donations above the limit are not tax-deductible.

## Two Methods for Tax Deduction

### 1. One-Stop Exception System (ワンストップ特例制度)
- Available if you donate to 5 or fewer municipalities in a year
- Available only if you do NOT need to file a tax return
- Submit a "One-Stop Exception Application Form" to each municipality by January 10
- Deduction applies only to resident tax (not income tax)

### 2. Through Tax Return (確定申告)
- Required if you donate to 6+ municipalities or file a tax return for other reasons
- Deduction applies to both income tax and resident tax
- Include donation receipts (寄附金受領証明書) with your tax return

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Calculate your limit carefully**: Use online simulators on the donation sites
2. **Start small**: If unsure about your limit, donate conservatively
3. **Deadline is December 31**: Donations must be completed by year-end for that tax year
4. **Keep receipts**: Save all donation receipts for tax filing
5. **Popular return gifts**: wagyu beef, seafood, rice, fruits, local crafts
6. **One-stop deadline**: Application must reach the municipality by January 10
