# Crypto Tax Overview

## Miscellaneous income

For many individuals in Japan, gains from crypto assets are treated as miscellaneous income rather than capital gains from listed securities. The tax result depends on the transaction type, taxpayer activity, and records. Business treatment is possible only when facts support it.

## Taxable events

Taxable events can include selling crypto for yen, exchanging one crypto asset for another, using crypto to buy goods or services, receiving rewards, or earning income through related services. Moving assets between your own wallets is usually a record-keeping event rather than a sale.

## Record keeping

Keep exchange statements, wallet addresses, trade histories, yen values, fees, and dates. If using overseas exchanges or self-custody wallets, export records regularly. Reconstructing multi-year transaction history after an exchange closes can be difficult.

## Caution

Crypto tax calculations can become complex when there are many trades, DeFi activity, forks, airdrops, or foreign exchange effects. Use calculation tools carefully and keep the assumptions. For large or unclear positions, consult the tax office or a tax accountant.
