# Donation Deductions

## Eligible donations

Some donations to national or local governments, approved public-interest organizations, schools, and certain charities may qualify for income-tax treatment. Eligibility depends on the recipient and documentation, not only on the donor's intention.

## Furusato nozei vs other donations

Furusato nozei is a donation system to local governments with separate resident-tax handling and optional return gifts. Other donations may follow different income-tax or tax-credit rules. Do not assume all charity payments work like furusato nozei.

## How to claim

Employees may use the furusato nozei one-stop process when eligible, or file a final tax return. Other donation deductions are usually claimed on a return with supporting documents. If you file a return, include all donation information there rather than relying on one-stop handling.

## Receipt handling

Keep donation receipts, certificates, and online confirmations. The receipt should show the donor, recipient, date, and amount. If the donor name differs from your tax records because of romanization or marriage, keep documents that explain the connection.
