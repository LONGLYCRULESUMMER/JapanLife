# Social Insurance and Tax

## Different systems

Social insurance and tax are related but separate systems. Health insurance, pension, employment insurance, income tax, and resident tax can use different offices, calculation methods, and payment schedules. A change reported to one system may not update all others automatically.

## Year-end adjustment documents

Social insurance premiums paid through payroll are usually reflected by the employer. Premiums paid personally, such as National Pension or National Health Insurance in some situations, may need certificates or payment records for deduction purposes during year-end adjustment or final filing.

## Income walls

Part-time workers and dependents often hear about income walls. Some thresholds relate to tax deductions, while others relate to social insurance enrollment or dependent status. The practical result depends on employer rules, household situation, and the specific program.

## Resident notes

Foreign residents should keep pension, health insurance, and tax records together. These documents may be needed for visa renewal, permanent residence, pension claims, or departure procedures. Ask the relevant office which system a notice belongs to before assuming it is a tax bill.
