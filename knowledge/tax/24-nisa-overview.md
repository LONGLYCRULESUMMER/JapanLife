# NISA Overview

## Tax-free investment idea

NISA is a Japanese tax-exempt investment account system for eligible residents. Investment gains and dividends inside the account can receive tax-free treatment when the account and products meet the rules. It is an investment wrapper, not a guaranteed return.

## Growth and tsumitate investment枠

The current NISA framework includes a growth investment枠 and a tsumitate investment枠. The growth side is used for a wider range of listed investments, while the tsumitate side focuses on long-term, regular investment products that meet official criteria.

## Not a deduction

NISA contributions are not an income deduction. Putting money into NISA does not reduce taxable salary or business income. The tax advantage is on eligible investment income inside the account, such as gains or dividends, rather than on the contribution itself.

## Resident notes

NISA is designed for residents in Japan. Moving overseas, changing tax residence, or using foreign securities accounts can affect eligibility and procedures. Confirm account handling with the financial institution before leaving Japan.
