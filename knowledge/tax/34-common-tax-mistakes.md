# Common Tax Mistakes

## Side income

Employees sometimes assume payroll withholding covers all income. Side jobs, freelance projects, platform income, royalties, and overseas payments may need separate review. Keep records even when the amount is small, because classification and filing rules depend on the facts.

## Overseas dependents

Deductions for overseas relatives require more documentation than domestic dependents. Missing relationship documents, remittance records, or translations can delay year-end adjustment or filing. Collect records during the year instead of asking family members at the last minute.

## Resident tax lag

Resident tax is billed after the income year. A job change, salary increase, or departure from Japan can create a bill later than expected. Budget for resident tax separately from national income tax refunds or payments.

## Missing documents

Many errors come from missing withholding slips, donation receipts, insurance certificates, loan balance certificates, or foreign statements. Use a tax-year folder and save e-Tax confirmations, payment receipts, and filed returns so future corrections and certificate requests are easier.
