# Tax Filing Overview for Foreign Residents in Japan

## Who Needs to File a Tax Return (確定申告)?

Japan uses a self-assessment system for income tax. You may need to file a final tax return (確定申告 / kakutei shinkoku) if:

1. **Employees with single employer**: Generally NO filing needed if your employer handles year-end adjustment (年末調整). However, you MUST file if:
   - Your annual salary exceeds ¥20,000,000
   - You have side income exceeding ¥200,000
   - You received income from two or more employers
   - You want to claim deductions not covered by year-end adjustment (medical expenses, etc.)

2. **Self-employed / Freelancers**: MUST file if income exceeds ¥480,000 (basic deduction)

3. **Leaving Japan mid-year**: MUST file a departure tax return before leaving

## Tax Residency Status

Your tax obligation depends on your residency classification:

### Non-Permanent Resident (非永住者)
- In Japan for less than 5 years out of the last 10 years
- Taxed on: Japan-sourced income + foreign income remitted to Japan
- Most expats on work visas fall into this category initially

### Permanent Resident (永住者) for Tax Purposes
- In Japan for 5+ years out of the last 10 years
- Taxed on: Worldwide income (same as Japanese nationals)
- Note: This is different from immigration permanent residency

### Non-Resident (非居住者)
- Not domiciled in Japan
- Taxed only on Japan-sourced income at flat 20.42%

## Tax Year and Filing Period

- Tax year: January 1 to December 31
- Filing period: February 16 to March 15 of the following year
- Late filing: subject to penalties (延滞税) and surcharges (加算税)
- Extensions available in limited circumstances

## Filing Methods

1. **e-Tax (電子申告)**: Online filing via My Number Card + card reader or smartphone
2. **Paper filing by mail**: Download forms from NTA website, mail to your local tax office
3. **In-person at tax office**: Visit during filing period; many offer English support in major cities

## Key Documents Needed

- Certificate of Income and Tax Withheld (源泉徴収票) from employer
- My Number Card or notification card
- Bank account details for refund
- Receipts for claimed deductions
- Passport (for tax treaty claims)
