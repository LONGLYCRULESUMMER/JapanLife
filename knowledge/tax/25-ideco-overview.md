# iDeCo Tax Overview

## Contribution deduction

iDeCo is an individual defined contribution pension system. Eligible contributions can generally be treated as income deductions, which may reduce taxable income for income tax and resident tax purposes. Contribution limits and eligibility depend on pension category and workplace plans.

## Investment gains

Assets inside iDeCo are invested in products selected by the participant. Investment gains inside the account receive tax-favored treatment while they remain in the pension account. Investment risk remains with the participant.

## Receiving benefits

Benefits are usually received at retirement age or later, either as a lump sum, pension-style payments, or a combination depending on plan rules. Tax treatment at receipt differs from the contribution stage and depends on how benefits are paid.

## Eligibility notes

Eligibility can be affected by employment status, public pension enrollment, age, and employer plan participation. Foreign residents should also consider expected length of stay in Japan because iDeCo assets are generally intended for retirement and are not a short-term savings account.
