# Invoice System for Freelancers

## Qualified invoice issuer

Japan's Invoice System allows registered qualified invoice issuers to issue invoices that buyers can use for consumption-tax input credits. Registration gives the business a registration number and changes how invoices need to be written and preserved.

## When registration matters

Registration matters most when business clients need qualified invoices. A freelancer who registers may become responsible for consumption-tax filing and payment even if they were previously tax-exempt. The decision affects pricing, administration, and client relationships.

## Client-side implications

Business clients may prefer registered suppliers because qualified invoices help their own consumption-tax calculations. Some clients ask for a registration number during vendor onboarding. If you are not registered, explain your status clearly and keep the commercial terms documented.

## Tips

Do not register only because a form asks for a number. Compare client needs, expected sales, pricing, and administrative burden. If you register, update invoice templates, accounting settings, contracts, and document retention procedures before issuing invoices.
