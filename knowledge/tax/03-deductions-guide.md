# Tax Deductions Guide for Foreign Residents in Japan

## Personal Deductions (人的控除)

### Basic Deduction (基礎控除)
- ¥480,000 for taxable income up to ¥24,000,000
- Gradually reduced above ¥24,000,000
- Zero above ¥25,000,000
- Everyone gets this automatically

### Spouse Deduction (配偶者控除)
- Up to ¥380,000 if spouse's income is ¥480,000 or less
- Spouse must be a resident of Japan (exceptions for tax treaty countries)
- Special Spouse Deduction available for spouse income up to ¥1,330,000

### Dependent Deduction (扶養控除)
- General dependents (16-18, 23+): ¥380,000 each
- Specified dependents (19-22, typically university age): ¥630,000 each
- Elderly dependents (70+): ¥480,000 (¥580,000 if living together)
- Dependents overseas: additional documentation required since 2023 revision

## Insurance Deductions (保険料控除)

### Social Insurance Premium Deduction (社会保険料控除)
- Full amount of social insurance premiums paid is deductible
- Includes: health insurance, pension, employment insurance
- For employees: automatically deducted from salary and included in year-end adjustment
- For self-employed: deduct the full amount of NHI and National Pension premiums paid

### Life Insurance Premium Deduction (生命保険料控除)
- Up to ¥120,000 total deduction
- Three categories: general life insurance, nursing care insurance, individual pension
- Maximum ¥40,000 per category for new contracts (post-2012)

### Earthquake Insurance Premium Deduction (地震保険料控除)
- Up to ¥50,000 deduction
- Covers premiums for earthquake insurance on your residence

## Expense-Based Deductions

### Medical Expense Deduction (医療費控除)
- Deduct medical expenses exceeding ¥100,000 (or 5% of income if lower)
- Maximum deduction: ¥2,000,000
- Includes: hospital fees, prescription drugs, dental, transportation to hospital
- Keep all receipts; can use the simplified "Medical Expense Notification" from insurer
- Alternative: Self-Medication Tax System — deduct ¥12,000+ of qualifying OTC drugs (max ¥88,000)

### Donation Deduction (寄附金控除)
- Includes Furusato Nozei donations
- Deduction = Total donations - ¥2,000
- Also applicable for donations to certified NPOs and universities

## Employment Income Deduction (給与所得控除)

This is automatically calculated based on salary amount:

| Annual Salary | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Up to ¥1,625,000 | ¥550,000 |
| ¥1,625,001 - ¥1,800,000 | Salary × 40% - ¥100,000 |
| ¥1,800,001 - ¥3,600,000 | Salary × 30% + ¥80,000 |
| ¥3,600,001 - ¥6,600,000 | Salary × 20% + ¥440,000 |
| ¥6,600,001 - ¥8,500,000 | Salary × 10% + ¥1,100,000 |
| Over ¥8,500,000 | ¥1,950,000 (cap) |

## Tax Treaty Benefits

Many countries have tax treaties with Japan that may provide:
- Reduced withholding tax rates on dividends, interest, royalties
- Exemption for short-term employment (typically 183-day rule)
- Teacher/researcher exemptions (2-3 year exemption for some countries)
- Student exemptions

To claim treaty benefits, file a Tax Treaty Application Form with your employer or tax office.
