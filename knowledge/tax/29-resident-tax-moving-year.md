# Resident Tax in Moving Years

## January 1 rule

Resident tax is generally assessed by the municipality where you have an address on January 1. It is based on the previous year's income, so bills arrive with a delay. This timing surprises people who move, change jobs, or leave Japan.

## Moving within Japan

If you move within Japan, the municipality where you lived on January 1 generally handles that year's resident tax. Update your address with the local office and employer so notices and payroll withholding go to the right place.

## Leaving Japan

Leaving Japan does not automatically cancel resident tax already assessed or to be assessed from prior-year income. Before departure, confirm whether the remaining amount will be paid in a lump sum, through an employer, or by a tax representative.

## Payment reminders

Resident-tax notices may arrive after a move. Keep mail forwarding, employer contact, and municipality contact information current. Late payment can create issues when requesting tax payment certificates for immigration or administrative procedures.
