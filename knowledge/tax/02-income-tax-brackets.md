# Japan Income Tax Brackets and Rates (2025)

## Progressive Tax Rate Table

Japan uses a progressive income tax system with the following brackets applied to taxable income:

| Taxable Income | Tax Rate | Deduction Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ¥1,950,000 | 5% | ¥0 |
| ¥1,950,001 to ¥3,300,000 | 10% | ¥97,500 |
| ¥3,300,001 to ¥6,950,000 | 20% | ¥427,500 |
| ¥6,950,001 to ¥9,000,000 | 23% | ¥636,000 |
| ¥9,000,001 to ¥18,000,000 | 33% | ¥1,536,000 |
| ¥18,000,001 to ¥40,000,000 | 40% | ¥2,796,000 |
| Over ¥40,000,000 | 45% | ¥4,796,000 |

Formula: Income Tax = Taxable Income × Tax Rate - Deduction Amount

## Reconstruction Special Income Tax (復興特別所得税)

An additional 2.1% surtax on your income tax amount, applied from 2013 through 2037. This was introduced to fund reconstruction after the 2011 earthquake.

Total tax = Income Tax + (Income Tax × 2.1%)

## Resident Tax (住民税)

In addition to national income tax, residents pay local resident tax:
- Prefectural tax: 4%
- Municipal tax: 6%
- Total: 10% of taxable income (flat rate)

Resident tax is assessed based on the previous year's income and paid in the current year (June through May). This means you may owe resident tax even after leaving Japan.

## Example Calculation

Employee earning ¥5,000,000 annual salary:

1. Employment Income Deduction: ¥5,000,000 × 20% + ¥440,000 = ¥1,440,000
2. Adjusted Income: ¥5,000,000 - ¥1,440,000 = ¥3,560,000
3. Basic Deduction: ¥480,000
4. Social Insurance (approx 15%): ¥750,000
5. Taxable Income: ¥3,560,000 - ¥480,000 - ¥750,000 = ¥2,330,000
6. Income Tax: ¥2,330,000 × 10% - ¥97,500 = ¥135,500
7. Reconstruction Tax: ¥135,500 × 2.1% = ¥2,845
8. Total National Tax: ¥138,345
9. Resident Tax: ¥2,330,000 × 10% = ¥233,000
10. Total Tax Burden: ¥371,345 (effective rate: ~7.4%)
