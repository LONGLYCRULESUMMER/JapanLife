# Year-End Adjustment (年末調整) Guide

## What is Year-End Adjustment?

Year-end adjustment (年末調整 / nenmatsu chosei) is the process by which your employer calculates your final tax liability for the year and adjusts the amount of income tax withheld from your salary.

If your employer handles year-end adjustment correctly, you typically do NOT need to file a separate tax return (確定申告).

## When Does It Happen?

- November-December: Employer distributes declaration forms
- December: Employer calculates final tax and adjusts your December (or January) paycheck
- January: Employer provides Certificate of Income (源泉徴収票)

## Forms You Need to Submit

Your employer will ask you to fill out these forms (in Japanese):

### 1. Declaration of Deduction for Dependents (扶養控除等申告書)
- List spouse and dependents who qualify for deductions
- Include overseas dependents (additional documentation required)

### 2. Declaration of Insurance Premium Deductions (保険料控除申告書)
- Life insurance premiums (生命保険料)
- Earthquake insurance premiums (地震保険料)
- Social insurance premiums paid privately (国民年金, 国民健康保険)

### 3. Basic Deduction Declaration (基礎控除申告書)
- Confirms your basic deduction eligibility
- Combined with spouse deduction and income adjustment forms

## What Year-End Adjustment Covers

- Basic deduction (基礎控除)
- Employment income deduction (給与所得控除)
- Dependent deductions (扶養控除)
- Spouse deduction (配偶者控除)
- Social insurance premium deductions
- Life and earthquake insurance deductions
- Housing loan deduction (from 2nd year onward)

## What Year-End Adjustment Does NOT Cover

You must file a separate tax return (確定申告) to claim:
- Medical expense deduction (医療費控除)
- Furusato Nozei (if filing tax return method)
- First year of housing loan deduction
- Donation deductions other than Furusato Nozei
- Tax treaty benefits
- Income from side jobs/multiple employers

## What to Check on Your 源泉徴収票

After year-end adjustment, review your Certificate of Income and Tax Withheld:
- Payment amount (支払金額): your total annual salary
- Taxable income after deductions (給与所得控除後の金額)
- Income deduction total (所得控除の額の合計額)
- Tax withheld (源泉徴収税額): total income tax paid

If the tax withheld is more than what you owe, you receive a refund in your December paycheck.
If less, the difference is deducted from your December paycheck.
