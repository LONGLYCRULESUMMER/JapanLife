# Dependent Deduction (扶養控除)

The dependent deduction lowers your taxable income when you financially support qualifying family
members. It is one of the most useful deductions for foreign residents who support a spouse,
children, or relatives.

## Who Qualifies as a Dependent

A qualifying dependent (扶養親族) generally must:

- Be a relative you support (by blood or marriage), living with you or supported by you.
- Have annual income of **¥480,000 or less** (equivalent to salary income of about
  **¥1,030,000 or less** — the well-known "¥1.03 million wall").
- Not be filed as a dependent by another taxpayer.

A spouse is handled separately through the **spouse deduction (配偶者控除)** or **special spouse
deduction (配偶者特別控除)**, which phase out as the spouse's income rises.

## Deduction Amounts

| Dependent type | Approx. deduction (income tax) |
|---|---|
| General dependent (16+) | ~¥380,000 |
| Specified dependent (ages 19–22) | ~¥630,000 |
| Elderly dependent (70+) | ~¥480,000 |
| Co-residing elderly parent (70+) | ~¥580,000 |

Children under 16 do not give an income-tax dependent deduction (they are covered by child
allowance instead), but they still matter for resident-tax calculations.

## Dependents Living Abroad (国外扶養親族)

You can claim relatives living overseas, but the rules are stricter. You must provide:

- **Relationship documents** (e.g., birth/marriage certificates, with translation), and
- **Remittance documents** proving you actually send money to support them.

For dependents aged 30–69 living abroad, additional conditions apply.

## How to Claim

- Through the **year-end adjustment (年末調整)** at your employer, by submitting the dependent
  declaration form, or
- Through a **tax return (確定申告)** if you file one.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. Keep **remittance records** (bank transfers, money-transfer receipts) if you support family
   abroad — they are required.
2. Watch the **¥1.03 million** income wall for a dependent, and the higher walls for a working
   spouse.
3. Update your dependent declaration whenever your family situation changes.
