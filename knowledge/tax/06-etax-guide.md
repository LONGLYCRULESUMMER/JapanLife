# e-Tax (電子申告) Online Filing Guide

## What is e-Tax?

e-Tax is Japan's electronic tax filing system operated by the National Tax Agency (NTA). It allows you to file your income tax return online without visiting a tax office.

## Requirements

### Option 1: My Number Card + Smartphone
- My Number Card (マイナンバーカード) with digital certificate
- Smartphone with NFC capability (iPhone 7+, Android with NFC)
- The e-Tax app or NTA's online filing page

### Option 2: My Number Card + IC Card Reader
- My Number Card
- IC card reader connected to your PC
- Windows or Mac with supported browser

### Option 3: ID and Password Method (暫定方式)
- Register ID and password at your local tax office (one-time, in person)
- Then file online using those credentials
- This method is being phased out; My Number Card method is recommended

## Step-by-Step Filing Process

### Preparation
1. Gather documents: 源泉徴収票, receipts, My Number, bank details
2. Access the NTA filing page: www.keisan.nta.go.jp
3. Select "Create New Return" and choose income type

### Filing
1. Enter income information from your 源泉徴収票
2. Enter deductions (medical, insurance, donations, etc.)
3. System automatically calculates your tax
4. Review the calculated return
5. Authenticate with My Number Card
6. Submit electronically

### After Filing
- Save or print the receipt confirmation (受付結果)
- Refund processed to your registered bank account (usually within 1-2 months)
- If you owe tax, pay by the deadline using bank transfer, credit card, or convenience store

## Language Support

The e-Tax system is primarily in Japanese. However:
- NTA provides an English guide at www.nta.go.jp/english
- The online calculation form has some English labels
- Tax offices in major cities (Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya) offer temporary English support during filing season
- Consider asking a Japanese-speaking friend or hiring a tax accountant for first-time filing

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Get My Number Card early**: Apply soon after arriving in Japan
2. **Use smartphone method**: Easiest for most people
3. **File early in February**: Avoid long lines and system congestion
4. **Save your data**: You can save progress and return later
5. **Keep PDF copy**: Download your submitted return for records
