# Housing Loan Deduction

## Basic idea

The housing loan deduction is a tax credit system for eligible home loans and qualifying residences. It is based on detailed conditions such as residence use, loan terms, floor area, income limits, and the year the home is occupied. Check the current rules before purchasing or refinancing.

## First-year tax return

The first year generally requires a final tax return even for employees whose salary is normally handled through year-end adjustment. Documents commonly include loan balance certificates, real-estate documents, and proof of residence. Prepare early because banks and registry documents can take time.

## Later year-end adjustment

After the first-year claim is accepted, employees may be able to use employer year-end adjustment for later years. Keep the tax office certificates and annual loan balance certificates because the employer needs them to process the deduction.

## Foreign resident notes

Foreign residents should confirm residence status, expected stay, mortgage terms, and name consistency across documents. If leaving Japan, renting out the home, or changing residence use, ask the tax office or a tax professional how the deduction is affected.
