# Tax Office Consultation

## Where to ask

Questions about national taxes can be directed to the local tax office, NTA consultation channels, or filing-season consultation sites. Municipal offices handle resident tax and local certificates. Check which office has authority before visiting.

## What to bring

Bring identification, My Number documents if needed, prior returns, withholding slips, notices, receipts, contracts, account statements, and a written summary of the question. For complex facts, a one-page timeline helps the staff understand the issue quickly.

## Interpretation limits

Tax office staff can explain procedures and official rules, but they may not provide broad planning advice or review every business decision. Answers can also depend on facts not visible in a short consultation. Keep notes of the date, office, and documents discussed.

## When to use a tax accountant

Consider a licensed tax accountant when you have business income, foreign income, securities or crypto complexity, past-year corrections, an audit notice, or high-value transactions. A tax accountant can review facts and prepare filings in a way general consultation may not cover.
