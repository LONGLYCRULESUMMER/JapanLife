# Documents for Final Tax Return

## Employment documents

Employees should gather Certificates of Income and Tax Withheld from every employer for the year. Also keep records for part-time work, retirement payments, stock compensation, or other employer-related income that may not be fully handled by one year-end adjustment.

## Deduction documents

Common deduction documents include insurance premium certificates, medical expense records, donation receipts, social insurance payment records, dependent documents, and housing loan documents. Keep originals or official electronic records according to the filing method.

## Foreign income documents

Foreign salary, dividends, interest, rental income, securities transactions, and tax paid overseas may need supporting records. Keep statements showing dates, currency, withholding, and exchange-rate assumptions. Tax-residency status affects what needs to be reported.

## Filing checklist

Before filing, confirm your address, My Number information, bank account for refunds, income categories, deductions, withholding, and payment method. Save a copy of the filed return, attachments, e-Tax messages, and proof of payment or refund.
