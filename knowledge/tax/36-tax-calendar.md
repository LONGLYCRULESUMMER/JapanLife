# Tax Calendar

## January to March

The individual income tax year closes on December 31, and final tax return season generally runs from mid-February to mid-March. Use January to collect withholding slips, deduction certificates, donation receipts, medical records, and business books.

## June resident tax

Resident-tax notices commonly arrive around June for the previous year's income. Employees may see payroll withholding start or change around this time. Self-employed people and others paying directly should check installment slips and due dates carefully.

## Year-end adjustment season

Employers usually collect year-end adjustment forms in autumn or early winter. Prepare dependent information, insurance certificates, spouse information, housing loan certificates for later years, and other documents requested by payroll.

## Freelancer deadlines

Freelancers should keep books monthly rather than waiting for filing season. Check deadlines for income tax, consumption tax if applicable, estimated tax payments, Blue Return applications, and invoice-system procedures. Calendar reminders are safer than relying on mailed notices.
