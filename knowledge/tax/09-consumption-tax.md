# Consumption Tax (消費税) in Japan

Consumption tax is Japan's value-added tax, charged on most goods and services. It combines a
national consumption tax and a local consumption tax into a single rate shown at the register.

## Rates

There are two rates:

- **Standard rate: 10%** — most goods and services.
- **Reduced rate: 8%** — food and non-alcoholic beverages (groceries and takeout), plus newspapers
  subscribed to twice or more per week.

The reduced rate creates a common surprise: **eating in vs taking out**.

| Purchase | Rate |
|---|---|
| Groceries at a supermarket | 8% |
| Takeout / delivery meal | 8% |
| Eat-in at a restaurant or cafe | 10% |
| Alcoholic drinks | 10% |
| Clothes, electronics, furniture | 10% |

## Tax-Included vs Tax-Excluded Pricing (税込 / 税抜)

Shops are generally required to show the **tax-included price (税込)**, but some still display the
pre-tax price (税抜) prominently with the final price in smaller text. Always check which one you
are reading before you pay.

## Tax-Free Shopping (免税)

Tax-free shopping applies to **short-term visitors**, not residents. If you live in Japan with a
residence card, you pay consumption tax like everyone else; you cannot use the visitor tax-free
counters.

## Consumption Tax for Businesses and Freelancers

If you run a business or freelance, consumption tax can affect you:

- Businesses with taxable sales over **¥10 million** in the base period are taxable businesses that
  must file and remit consumption tax.
- Smaller businesses may be **tax-exempt (免税事業者)**, but many clients now expect a qualified
  invoice.
- The **Invoice System (インボイス制度)** began in October 2023: to let clients claim input tax
  credits, you must register as a qualified invoice issuer and show your registration number.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. Remember the **eat-in 10% vs takeout 8%** difference when ordering.
2. Keep receipts — they show the tax breakdown.
3. As a resident you cannot use visitor tax-free shopping.
4. If you freelance, decide whether to register for the Invoice System; it affects your pricing.
