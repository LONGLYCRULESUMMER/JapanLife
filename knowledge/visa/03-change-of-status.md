# Change of Status of Residence (在留資格変更)

## When You Need a Change of Status
A Change of Status of Residence (在留資格変更許可申請) is required when your activity in
Japan changes to one not permitted by your current residence status. Common cases:
- Graduating from a Japanese school (Student / 留学) and starting full-time work.
- Switching from one work category to another (e.g. Instructor to Engineer/Specialist in
  Humanities/International Services).
- Moving from a work status to Spouse of a Japanese National (日本人の配偶者等) after marriage.

You must **not** begin the new activity until the change is approved.

## Where and How to Apply
Apply at the regional **Immigration Services Agency** office (地方出入国在留管理局) that covers
your registered address, or online through the Immigration Services Agency e-application system
if you are registered for it. A company or accredited agent may also file on your behalf.

## Required Documents (typical)
- Application for Change of Status of Residence (申請書)
- Passport and residence card (在留カード)
- ID photograph (4 cm x 3 cm)
- Documents proving the new activity, for example a certificate of employment, an employment
  contract, or a certificate of graduation
- Company documents (registration, financial statements) for work-status changes
- Proof of tax and pension payment may be requested

## Processing Time and Fee
Processing usually takes **2 weeks to 1 month**, sometimes longer in busy seasons such as spring.
If approved, a revenue stamp fee (currently ¥4,000 for the permit) is payable when you collect the
new residence card.

## Common Mistakes
- Starting the new job before approval.
- Applying after the current status has already expired.
- Activity mismatch between your degree/experience and the job (a frequent reason for denial).
