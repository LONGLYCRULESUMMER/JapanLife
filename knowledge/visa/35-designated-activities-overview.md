# Designated Activities Overview (特定活動)

Designated Activities is a flexible status for activities individually designated by the Minister of
Justice. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## What It Covers

This status is used for many special cases, such as job hunting after graduation, internships,
medical stays, domestic staff in limited situations, startup preparation, or other activities covered
by a specific designation.

## Application Process

The required route depends on the exact designated activity. Some cases use a COE, some use change of
status, and some require documents from a school, employer, host organization, local government, or
family sponsor.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if applicable.
- Written explanation of the designated activity and planned period.
- Contracts, school letters, host documents, financial support, or program approvals.
- Any designation-specific forms or certificates listed by immigration.

## Cautions

The permission is limited to the activity described in the designation. Do not assume that all
Designated Activities holders have the same work rights, renewal rules, or family options.
