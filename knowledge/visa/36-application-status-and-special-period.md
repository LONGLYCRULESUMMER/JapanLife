# Application Status and Special Period While Pending

If you apply to renew or change status before expiry, a special pending period may protect your stay
while immigration reviews the application. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## How the Special Period Works

When an eligible renewal or change application is accepted before the current period expires, the
resident may generally remain under the current status until a decision is made or for up to **2
months** after the original expiry date, whichever comes first.

## While Waiting

Keep the application receipt or online notice with your residence card. Continue following the
conditions of your current status. For a change-of-status case, do not start the new activity until
the change is approved.

## Documents to Keep

- Application receipt, postcard, online notice, or other proof of filing.
- Current passport and residence card.
- Copies of the submitted application and any additional-document requests.
- Employer, school, or family documents showing continued activity during review.

## Cautions

The special period is not an automatic extra visa for every situation. It ends when a decision is
made, and travel or re-entry plans can become complicated near expiry. Apply early and respond quickly
to document requests.
