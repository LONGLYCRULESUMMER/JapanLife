# Spouse or Child of Japanese National (日本人の配偶者等)

This status is for people closely related to a Japanese national. Its biggest advantage is that it
carries **no work restrictions** — unlike the Dependent visa.

## Who Is Eligible

- **Spouses** of Japanese nationals.
- **Biological children** of Japanese nationals.
- **Specially adopted children (特別養子)** of Japanese nationals.

A common-law relationship generally does not qualify; a legally registered marriage is required.

## Work Freedom

Holders may **work in any field with no restrictions** — there is no 28-hour cap and no need for
permission to engage in other activities. This is a major difference from the Dependent
(家族滞在) visa.

## Periods of Stay

Granted for **6 months, 1, 3, or 5 years**, renewable. Longer periods are typically granted as
your marriage and residence history become established.

## Documents

- Proof of the relationship: marriage registration / family register (戸籍謄本).
- Evidence of a **genuine, ongoing marriage** (photos, correspondence, a written statement).
- Income and tax documents of the household.

## After Divorce or Bereavement

If you divorce or your Japanese spouse passes away, you **must notify immigration within 14 days**.
Without a change of status or remarriage, the spouse status can be lost; in some cases a
**Long-Term Resident (定住者)** status may be granted, especially with children or long residence.

## Path to Permanent Residency

Spouses of Japanese nationals often qualify for **permanent residency** sooner than other
residents — commonly after about **3 years of marriage plus 1 year of residence** in Japan.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Report marital-status changes promptly** (within 14 days).
2. Keep evidence that your marriage is genuine — immigration looks for it at renewal.
3. The unrestricted work right makes this one of the most flexible statuses.
