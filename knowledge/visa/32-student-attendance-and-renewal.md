# Student Attendance and Renewal (留学)

Student status is for study at an approved educational institution in Japan. This guide is general
information, not legal or immigration advice.

## What Immigration Reviews

Renewal usually focuses on continued enrollment, attendance, academic progress, financial support,
and compliance with work limits. Poor attendance or unclear study purpose can weaken an application.

## Work Permission

Students need permission to engage in other activity before part-time work. The common limit is
**28 hours per week**, with different handling during long school vacations. Work must not interfere
with study.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card.
- Certificate of enrollment, transcript, attendance record, and expected graduation date.
- Bank, scholarship, sponsor, or part-time income documents showing support.
- Explanation and evidence if grades, attendance, or school changes need context.

## Cautions

Overwork, low attendance, unpaid fees, or unsupported school transfers can affect renewal. Keep school
records and report required organization changes on time.
