# Side Work and Permission to Engage in Other Activity (資格外活動許可)

Permission to engage in other activity allows certain paid activities outside your main residence
status. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who Uses It

Students and dependents often need this permission before part-time work. Work-status holders may
need individual permission for side work that is outside their approved status. The main activity must
remain the primary reason for staying in Japan.

## Application Process

Apply to immigration with details of the activity. Some permissions are broad, such as common student
or dependent part-time permission; others are tied to a specific side job or contract.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, passport, and residence card.
- Explanation of the side activity, hours, pay, location, and employer or client.
- Documents showing the activity will not interfere with your main status.
- School or employer documents if immigration requests them.

## Cautions

Do not start paid side work until permission is granted. Hour limits and prohibited work categories
can apply, and side work can affect renewal if it harms attendance, employment, or status compliance.
