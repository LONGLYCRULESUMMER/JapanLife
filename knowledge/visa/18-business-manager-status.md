# Business Manager Status (経営・管理)

Business Manager is for people who establish, manage, or administer a business in Japan. This guide
is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

This status may fit founders, executives, branch managers, and administrators who have a real role in
running a business in Japan. Immigration reviews whether the business has substance, continuity, and
a genuine management need.

## Application Process

Applicants may use a **COE** before entering Japan or apply for a change of status if already in
Japan. Renewals focus on whether the business is operating, properly documented, and able to support
the manager's stay.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if applicable.
- Corporate registration, articles, shareholder or investment records, and office lease.
- Business plan, contracts, invoices, financial statements, and tax filings when available.
- Evidence of your management role, compensation, staffing, and business scale.

## Cautions

A paper company, unclear office arrangement, or unsupported business plan can create problems.
Maintain bookkeeping, tax filings, contracts, and proof of operations. Requirements can vary by
business stage and facts.
