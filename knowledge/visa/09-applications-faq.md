# Visa Applications: Documents, Timelines, and Common Mistakes

## Documents You Almost Always Need
Across renewal, change of status, and most applications, prepare:
- The relevant application form (申請書)
- Passport and residence card (在留カード)
- ID photograph (4 cm x 3 cm), taken within the last 3 months
- Proof of your activity: certificate of employment, employment contract, or enrollment
- Proof of finances and tax: payslips, withholding slip (源泉徴収票), tax payment certificate
  (納税証明書), and resident-tax certificate (課税証明書) when requested

## Typical Processing Timelines
- Extension of period of stay: about **2 weeks to 1 month**.
- Change of status of residence: about **2 weeks to 1 month**, sometimes up to 2 months.
- Certificate of Eligibility (for bringing family or new hires from abroad): often **1 to 3
  months**.
- Permanent residence: commonly **4 months to over a year**.

Spring (February to April) is the busiest season; apply as early as your window allows. You may
apply to renew from **3 months before** your card expires.

## If Your Card Expires While a Decision Is Pending
If you applied for renewal or change before expiry, a special provision generally lets you stay
lawfully for up to **2 months** past the expiry date while the decision is pending.

## Common Mistakes That Cause Delays or Denials
- Starting a new job or activity before approval.
- Income or duties that do not match the status applied for.
- Missing pension / health-insurance payments (especially for permanent residence).
- Photos that are too old or the wrong size.
- Filing at the wrong regional immigration office for your address.
