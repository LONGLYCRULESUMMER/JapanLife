# Long-term Resident Status (定住者)

Long-term Resident is a status designated for specific humanitarian, family, or settled-life
situations. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It May Cover

Examples can include certain descendants of Japanese nationals, family-related cases, refugee-related
or humanitarian situations, and other cases designated by the Minister of Justice. Eligibility is
highly fact-specific.

## Application Process

Some applicants use a **COE** from outside Japan. Others apply for a change of status after a major
life event or based on an established situation in Japan. Renewals focus on continued residence,
stability, and compliance.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if applicable.
- Family-register, birth, marriage, divorce, custody, or relationship documents.
- Residence history, income, tax, school, medical, or humanitarian evidence as relevant.
- Explanation of why the case fits a Long-term Resident designation.

## Cautions

Long-term Resident is not the same as Permanent Resident and still has a period of stay. Because the
category depends heavily on facts and designations, avoid assuming eligibility from another person's
case.
