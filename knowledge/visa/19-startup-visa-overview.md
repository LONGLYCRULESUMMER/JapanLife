# Startup Visa Overview

Japan's startup visa programs help foreign entrepreneurs prepare to launch a business before meeting
all normal Business Manager requirements. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

Programs are usually run through approved local governments or national startup-support schemes. They
are intended for founders with a concrete business plan who need time in Japan to set up an office,
company, financing, customers, or other launch requirements.

## Application Process

The entrepreneur typically submits a startup plan to the participating authority first. If the plan is
accepted, the applicant uses that support to apply for the relevant status of residence and later
works toward Business Manager or another suitable status.

## Documents to Prepare

- Business plan, founder profile, schedule, and financial plan.
- Explanation of the product, market, customers, and expected Japan operations.
- Passport, photo, application forms, and proof of living expenses.
- Any documents requested by the local program, incubator, or support office.

## Cautions

Rules differ by municipality and program. A startup visa is usually a preparation path, not permanent
approval to run any business indefinitely. Track deadlines and the conditions needed to move to the
next status.
