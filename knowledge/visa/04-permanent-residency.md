# Permanent Residency (永住許可)

## What Permanent Residency Gives You
Permanent residency (永住者) removes the renewal cycle and activity restrictions of work statuses:
you can work in any field and no longer renew your period of stay. You still hold a residence card
and must report address changes; permanent residency can be revoked for serious violations.

## Standard Requirements
The general guideline for permanent residence permission is:
- **10 continuous years** of residence in Japan, with at least **5 years** on a work status
  (就労資格) or residence status (not as a student for the whole period).
- Good conduct (素行善良): no serious criminal or immigration violations.
- Independent livelihood (独立生計): stable income and assets to support yourself.
- Being of benefit to Japan, including proper payment of taxes, pension, and health insurance.

## Faster Tracks
- **Highly Skilled Professional (高度専門職)**: with 80+ points you may apply after **1 year**;
  with 70+ points after **3 years**.
- **Spouse of a Japanese national or permanent resident**: generally after **3 years of marriage
  and 1 year of residence** in Japan (married 3+ years, last 1+ year living in Japan).

## Documents That Are Commonly Checked
- Tax payment certificates (納税証明書) for the past several years
- Proof of pension and health-insurance payments (a frequent failure point)
- A guarantor (身元保証人), usually a Japanese national or permanent resident
- Reason statement, employment and income documents, residence history

## Common Mistakes
- Gaps or late payments in pension / health insurance.
- Long absences from Japan that break the "continuous residence" requirement.
- Applying close to a renewal or with an unstable income.
