# Short-term Stay Limitations (短期滞在)

Short-term stay is for temporary visits such as tourism, family visits, short business meetings, and
similar activities. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Permitted Purposes

Typical purposes include sightseeing, visiting relatives, attending meetings, market research,
contract discussions, conferences, or other short non-resident activities. The stay should remain
temporary and match the purpose explained at entry.

## Main Limits

Short-term stay does not create a medium- or long-term residence status, and residents usually do not
receive a residence card. Paid work in Japan is generally outside the purpose of this status unless a
specific rule clearly permits the activity.

## Documents to Prepare

- Passport, itinerary, return or onward travel plan, and proof of funds.
- Invitation letter, schedule, or business-meeting details when relevant.
- Documents showing the visit is temporary and consistent with the stated purpose.

## Cautions

Repeated long visits, job hunting that becomes work, or trying to live in Japan on short-term stays
can cause entry or renewal problems. Changing from short-term stay to another status is limited and
fact-specific.
