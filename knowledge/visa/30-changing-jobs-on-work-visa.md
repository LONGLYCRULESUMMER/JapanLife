# Changing Jobs on a Work Visa

Changing employers can be straightforward when the new role fits your current status, but the details
matter. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Check Status Fit

Compare the new duties, industry, employer, and contract with your current status of residence. A new
software or office-specialist role may fit an existing work status, while a different field or
business-manager role may require a change of status.

## Required Notifications

Many work-status holders must notify immigration within **14 days** after leaving or joining a
contracting organization. This notification is separate from permission to work and separate from the
next renewal application.

## Documents to Keep

- Resignation and new employment dates.
- New contract, job description, salary, and work location.
- New employer's company profile, registration, and financial documents if available.
- Tax, pension, and withholding records for renewal.

## Cautions

A notification does not guarantee that the new job is within your status. If uncertain, consider a
certificate of authorized employment or a change-of-status application before relying on the new role
for renewal.
