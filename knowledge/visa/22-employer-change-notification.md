# Employer Change Notification (所属機関等に関する届出)

Many work-status holders must notify immigration when their contracting organization changes. This
guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who Must Notify

This commonly applies when a resident on an employment-based status leaves an employer, joins a new
employer, or when the affiliated organization changes name, address, or ends operations. The filing
is generally due within **14 days** of the change.

## How to Notify

Notifications can usually be submitted online, by mail, or at a regional immigration office. The
notice reports the change; it is separate from a renewal, change-of-status application, or certificate
of authorized employment.

## Documents to Prepare

- Residence card information and personal details.
- Name, address, and contact details of the old and new organization.
- Date of resignation, termination, transfer, or new contract.
- Copies of notices or receipts for your records.

## Cautions

Notification does not automatically confirm that the new job fits your current status. If duties,
industry, or employment type change substantially, check whether a change of status or other filing is
needed.
