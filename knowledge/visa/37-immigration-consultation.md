# Immigration Consultation

Official consultation channels can help residents confirm procedures, forms, and where to apply. This
guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Where to Ask

The Immigration Services Agency provides consultation information through regional immigration
offices, information centers, and official support channels. Some services offer multilingual help or
refer residents to the right counter.

## Before Contacting

Prepare a short summary of your status, expiry date, nationality, current activity, and question.
Have your residence card, passport, application notices, and related documents nearby when calling or
visiting.

## Good Questions to Bring

- Which procedure applies: COE, renewal, change of status, notification, or permission?
- Which regional office or online system should be used?
- Which official form and supporting documents are required for the status category?
- How to handle a deadline, document request, or change in circumstances?

## Cautions

Official counters can explain procedures but may not represent you or prepare your case. For complex,
high-risk, or disputed matters, consider a qualified immigration lawyer or administrative scrivener.
Use current official sources and avoid unverified intermediaries.
