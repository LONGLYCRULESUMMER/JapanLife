# Spouse Status After Divorce or Bereavement

Residents whose status is based on a spouse must report major changes such as divorce or the spouse's
death. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who Must Notify

This applies to residents whose status depends on a marital relationship, such as Spouse of Japanese
National, Spouse of Permanent Resident, or Dependent as a spouse. Notification is generally required
within **14 days** of divorce or bereavement.

## Next Steps

After notification, review whether your current status still fits your situation. Depending on your
facts, you may need to prepare a change-of-status application, renewal explanation, or other route
before your current period of stay ends.

## Documents to Prepare

- Residence card, passport, and notification form information.
- Divorce certificate, death certificate, family-register record, or equivalent proof.
- Documents showing residence history, income, family situation, children, or work if applying for a
  different status.

## Cautions

A spouse-based status can become vulnerable if the spouse relationship has ended. Do not ignore the
notification deadline, and do not assume that the remaining card period guarantees future renewal.
Complex cases should be checked with an official or qualified professional.
