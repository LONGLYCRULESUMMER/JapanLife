# Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services (技術・人文知識・国際業務)

This common work status covers office, technical, humanities, and international-service work for a
Japanese organization. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

Typical roles include **software engineering**, systems work, accounting, marketing, planning,
translation, interpretation, language-related business, overseas trade, and similar specialist work.
The activity should match your education, professional background, or practical experience.

## Application Process

New arrivals usually use a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** sponsored by the employer. Residents
already in Japan may apply for a **change of status**, and current holders apply for renewal before
their period of stay expires.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if already in Japan.
- Employment contract or offer letter showing duties, salary, and work location.
- Degree certificates, transcripts, or proof of relevant work experience.
- Employer documents such as registration, financial statements, and business outline.

## Cautions

The approved activity is not a general work permit. Unrelated manual labor, a major job-duty change,
or a move to a different status category may require a new application. Notify immigration after
leaving or changing employers when notification rules apply.
