# Re-entry Permit (再入国許可)

If you hold a residence status and plan to leave Japan temporarily, you usually need to preserve
your status so you can return. There are two mechanisms.

## Special Re-entry Permit (みなし再入国許可)

If you will return **within 1 year** of departure, you normally do **not** need a paper permit.
Simply present your residence card at the airport and **tick the special re-entry box** on the
embarkation/disembarkation card (ED card). This is free.

- Limit: re-entry **within 1 year** (or by your residence card's expiry, whichever is earlier).
- Permanent residents: within the validity period of the residence card.
- A special re-entry permit **cannot be extended** while you are abroad.

## Regular Re-entry Permit (再入国許可)

If you will be away **longer than 1 year**, apply for a regular re-entry permit **before** leaving:

- Valid up to **5 years** (up to **6 years** for permanent residents), not exceeding your period of
  stay.
- Single or multiple-entry; a fee applies.
- Apply at a regional immigration office with your residence card and passport.

## Why It Matters

If you leave Japan **without** the correct re-entry arrangement, your residence status — including
permanent residency — can be **lost**, and you would have to start the visa process over.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. For trips **over 1 year**, get the **regular** re-entry permit before you depart.
2. For short trips, remember to **check the special re-entry box** when leaving.
3. Watch the expiry date on your **residence card** — special re-entry cannot outlast it.
