# Residence Card (在留カード)

## What the Residence Card Is
The residence card (在留カード) is issued to mid-to-long-term residents and proves your identity,
status of residence, period of stay, and work eligibility. You must **carry it at all times** and
present it to immigration officers or police on request.

## How You Receive It
- At major airports (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, New Chitose, Hiroshima, Fukuoka) the card is
  issued on arrival.
- At other ports it is mailed to your address **after you submit your moving-in notification
  (転入届)** at the municipal office. This is why registering your address quickly matters.

## If Your Card Is Lost, Stolen, or Damaged
- Report loss or theft and obtain a police report (遺失届) or, for theft, a theft report.
- Apply for **reissue (在留カードの再交付)** at a regional Immigration Services Agency office
  **within 14 days** of noticing the loss (or on return to Japan).
- Damaged or defaced cards can also be reissued.

## Updating the Card
The card is reissued or updated when you:
- Renew or change your status (a new card is printed).
- Change your name, nationality, or similar registered details (within 14 days).
- Change address — handled through the municipal office, which updates the back of the card.

## When You Leave Japan
If you give up your residence and leave Japan permanently, return the residence card to an
immigration officer at the airport or by mail to the Immigration Services Agency.
