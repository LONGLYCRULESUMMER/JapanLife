# Visa Renewal and Change of Status

Keeping your residence status valid is essential. Renew on time and make sure your status matches
your real activity in Japan.

## When to Apply (在留期間更新)

Apply to renew at the **Immigration Services Agency (入管)** from **about 3 months before** your
residence card expires. If your application is still under examination when the card expires, you
may stay under a special provision for **up to 2 months** past the expiry date while it is decided.
Do not let the card lapse without applying — overstaying is serious.

## Required Documents

- Application form for extension of period of stay.
- Passport and residence card (在留カード).
- A recent photo.
- Proof of your activity and finances: for workers, an **employment certificate (在職証明書)** and
  **income/tax certificates (課税証明書・納税証明書)**; company documents may be requested.

## Processing Time

Typically **2 weeks to 1 month**. You will be notified to collect the new residence card and pay a
fee (by revenue stamp).

## Period Granted

You may receive **1, 3, or 5 years** (or shorter). The length depends on your history: stable
employment, sufficient income, and a clean record of **tax and pension payment** lead to longer
periods.

## Change of Status (在留資格変更)

To change status (e.g., from **Student** to **Engineer/Specialist**), file a **Change of Status of
Residence** application. **Do not start the new activity** (such as full-time work) until it is
approved.

## Address Change (住居地変更届)

After moving, **register your new address at the ward office within 14 days**. This updates the
address on your residence card and reports it to immigration — you do not file address changes with
immigration separately.

## Common Reasons for Trouble

- **Unpaid tax or pension** — increasingly checked at renewal and PR.
- **Activity not matching status** — e.g., working outside your field.
- **Overstays** or late notifications.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Pay your pension and tax** — non-payment is a leading cause of trouble at renewal.
2. **Apply early** (up to 3 months before expiry).
3. Keep copies of every application and receipt.
