# Highly-Skilled Professional (高度専門職)

The Highly-Skilled Professional status rewards qualified foreign professionals with a points-based
fast track to long stays and permanent residency. It is one of the most advantageous statuses if
you qualify.

## Points System

You are scored on a **points table** covering academic background, professional career, annual
income, age, Japanese-language ability, and bonus items (e.g., degrees from top universities).
Reaching **70 points** qualifies you for Highly-Skilled Professional (i) status (高度専門職1号).

The activity categories are:

- (a) advanced academic research,
- (b) advanced specialized/technical work,
- (c) advanced business management.

## Benefits

- A **5-year** period of stay.
- **Fast-track permanent residency**: apply after **3 years**, or after **1 year** if you hold
  **80 points or more**.
- Your **spouse can work** under relaxed conditions.
- Permission to engage in **multiple activities** across categories.
- Under conditions, you may bring a **parent** or **domestic helper**.

## 1号 vs 2号

After three years on Highly-Skilled Professional (i) (高度専門職1号), you can move to
**(ii) status (高度専門職2号)**, which allows almost unrestricted activities and an effectively
indefinite period of stay.

## How to Apply

Apply with a **Certificate of Eligibility** (for new arrivals) or a **change of status**, including
the official **points calculation sheet** and supporting evidence (diplomas, contracts, income
statements).

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Recalculate your points** each year — income and a master's/PhD weigh heavily and your score
   can rise.
2. Hitting **80 points** shortens the PR wait to just one year.
3. Keep certificates and income proof organized; the points must be evidenced.
