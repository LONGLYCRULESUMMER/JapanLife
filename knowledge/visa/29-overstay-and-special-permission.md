# Overstay and Special Permission Overview (在留特別許可)

Overstay is serious and can lead to detention, deportation procedures, and entry bans. This guide is
general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## What Special Permission Is

Special permission for residence is a discretionary decision made during immigration procedures. It
may be considered where there are humanitarian, family, social, or other compelling circumstances,
but it is not an entitlement.

## Process Overview

A person who has overstayed should address the situation with immigration rather than hiding. The
procedure may involve investigation, hearings, departure-order considerations, or a request for
special permission depending on the facts.

## Documents That May Matter

- Identity documents, residence history, and explanation of the overstay.
- Family records, children's school records, medical or humanitarian materials if relevant.
- Work, tax, community, and conduct records that accurately describe life in Japan.

## Cautions

Do not rely on informal advice or false documents. Because consequences are severe and facts matter,
consider official consultation or a qualified professional before deciding how to proceed.
