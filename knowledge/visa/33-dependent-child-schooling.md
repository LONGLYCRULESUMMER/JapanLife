# Dependent Child Schooling

Children on Dependent status can live in Japan with a supporting resident and attend school. This
guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Status Basics

Dependent status is based on the family relationship and the supporter's ability to maintain the
child in Japan. School attendance alone is not the immigration basis, but school records can help show
stable family life during renewals.

## School Enrollment

Families commonly contact the local municipal office or school board for public elementary or junior
high school procedures. International schools and private schools set their own admissions and fee
requirements.

## Documents to Keep

- Child's passport, residence card, and family relationship documents.
- Supporter's residence card, income, employment or school records, and tax documents.
- School enrollment certificates, attendance records, tuition records, and address records.

## Cautions

If the supporter loses status, changes status, or leaves Japan, the child's status may also be
affected. Older children who want part-time work need to check permission rules before working.
