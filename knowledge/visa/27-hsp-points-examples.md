# Highly Skilled Professional Point Examples

Highly Skilled Professional (HSP) status uses a points table for advanced research, specialized work,
and business management. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## How Points Work

Applicants calculate points from the official table and submit evidence for each item. Common factors
include academic background, professional experience, annual income, age, Japanese ability, research
achievements, and bonus categories.

## Example Profiles

A young engineer with a master's degree, several years of experience, strong income, and Japanese
language evidence may reach the threshold. A researcher may rely more on a PhD, publications, and
research achievements. A manager may rely on business experience and compensation.

## Documents to Prepare

- Official points calculation sheet for the correct HSP activity category.
- Diplomas, transcripts, employment certificates, and income documents.
- Japanese-language certificates, research records, patents, or bonus-point evidence.
- Employment contract or business-management documents matching the claimed category.

## Cautions

Only evidenced points count. Recalculate before each application because income, age, job category,
and bonus items can change. The official table and immigration review control the result, not an
informal estimate.
