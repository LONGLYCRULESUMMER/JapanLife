# Student and Work Visas (留学・就労ビザ)

## Student Status (留学)
The Student status (留学) is for people enrolled at a Japanese university, college, language
school, or similar institution. Key points:
- Period of stay is granted in increments tied to your program (e.g. 1 year 3 months, 2 years).
- Students may **not** work under the Student status itself.

### Part-Time Work Permission (資格外活動許可)
To work part-time, a student must obtain Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted
(資格外活動許可). With it you may work up to **28 hours per week** (up to 8 hours/day during long
school holidays). Work in adult-entertainment businesses is prohibited regardless of permission.

## Work Status (就労資格)
The most common work status is **Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services**
(技術・人文知識・国際業務). It requires:
- A job offer whose duties match your university degree or documented experience.
- An employer that can show stable operations.

Validity is typically **1, 3, or 5 years** and is tied to engaging in the permitted work. Other
work statuses include Instructor, Intra-company Transferee, Skilled Labor, and Highly Skilled
Professional.

## Moving From Student to Work
New graduates apply for a **Change of Status of Residence** (see the change-of-status guide).
Apply early — ideally in the autumn/winter before an April start — because spring is the busiest
season at immigration.

## Common Mistakes
- A student working over 28 hours/week without holiday rules applying.
- Taking a job unrelated to your field of study after graduation.
- Letting the Student status lapse before the work change is approved.
