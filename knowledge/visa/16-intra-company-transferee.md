# Intra-company Transferee (企業内転勤)

The Intra-company Transferee status is for employees transferred to a Japanese office within the
same corporate group. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

It generally applies when an employee of an overseas parent, subsidiary, branch, or affiliate is sent
to Japan to perform work similar to **Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services**.
The transfer should be internal to the group, not a new unrelated local hire.

## Application Process

The Japanese receiving entity usually prepares a **COE** application before the transfer. If the
employee is already in Japan, a change-of-status application may be needed before starting the new
activity.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if applicable.
- Transfer order or assignment letter describing period, duties, salary, and location.
- Documents showing the relationship between the overseas and Japanese entities.
- Proof of prior employment, professional background, and the Japanese entity's business.

## Cautions

This status is tied to the transfer arrangement and the approved activity. Moving to a different
Japanese employer, local permanent role, or unrelated work may require a change of status. Keep
assignment records for renewal.
