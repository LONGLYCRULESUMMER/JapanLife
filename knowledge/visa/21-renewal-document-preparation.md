# Renewal Document Preparation (在留期間更新)

Renewal extends your current period of stay when your activity in Japan continues. This guide is
general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## When to Prepare

Start early, especially if you need employer, school, tax, or family documents. Applications are
generally accepted from about **3 months before** the expiry date, and a special pending period may
apply if you file before expiry.

## Core Documents

- Application form, recent photo, passport, and residence card.
- Documents proving your current activity: employment, school, family, or business records.
- Income, tax, pension, or financial-support documents when relevant.
- Organization documents requested for your status category.

## Process

Submit the application to immigration or through an eligible online route. Keep the receipt or
application notice. If approved, you collect the new residence card and pay the fee by revenue stamp.

## Cautions

Renewal is not only a paperwork check. Immigration may review whether your activity matches your
status, whether income is stable, and whether taxes and notifications are up to date.
