# Certificate of Eligibility (COE) Process

A Certificate of Eligibility is a pre-screening document used for many medium- and long-term stays in
Japan. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## What a COE Does

The COE shows that immigration has reviewed the planned activity and basic landing conditions. It is
commonly used for work, study, family, and business statuses before applying for a visa at a Japanese
embassy or consulate.

## Application Process

A sponsor or representative in Japan often files the COE application with a regional immigration
office. After issuance, the COE is sent to the applicant, who uses it for the visa application and
then presents required documents when landing in Japan.

## Documents to Prepare

- COE application form, photo, and passport copy.
- Documents proving the planned activity, such as employment, school, family, or business records.
- Sponsor documents, financial evidence, and organization materials.
- Translations or explanations when documents are not straightforward.

## Cautions

A COE is not the same as a visa and does not guarantee landing. Check its validity period, keep the
planned activity consistent, and update the application if major facts change before entry.
