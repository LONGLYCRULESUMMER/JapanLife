# Family Stay and Dependent Details (家族滞在)

Dependent status allows certain family members to live in Japan with a resident who supports them.
This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

Dependent status generally covers the **spouse and children** of a foreign resident with an eligible
work, student, or similar status. The supporter must be able to maintain the family financially in
Japan.

## Application Process

Families outside Japan commonly use a **COE**. A family member already in Japan may need a
change-of-status application. Renewals usually follow the supporter status and the family's continued
relationship and support.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if applicable.
- Marriage certificate, birth certificate, or family-register documents.
- Supporter's residence card, employment or school records, and income or savings evidence.
- Translations when relationship documents are not in Japanese.

## Cautions

Dependent status is not a full work status. Part-time work requires permission to engage in other
activity and is usually limited. Divorce, separation, loss of support, or the supporter's status
change may require prompt action.
