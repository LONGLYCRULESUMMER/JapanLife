# Specified Skilled Worker (特定技能)

Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) is a work status for designated industries facing labor shortages.
This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Who It Covers

SSW Type 1 is for workers with confirmed skills and Japanese ability in an eligible sector. SSW Type
2 is for more advanced skill levels in eligible fields and may allow broader family accompaniment.
Sector, job type, and skill-test rules matter.

## Application Process

Applicants usually prove eligibility through sector skill tests, Japanese-language tests, or a
completed technical-intern path where accepted. The employer and support organization prepare the
employment terms, support plan, and COE or change-of-status documents.

## Documents to Prepare

- Application form, photo, passport, and residence card if already in Japan.
- Skill-test, Japanese-test, or technical-intern completion evidence.
- Employment contract, explanation of wages, and working conditions.
- Support plan and employer documents required for the specific sector.

## Cautions

SSW permission is limited to approved sectors and duties. A job change may require new procedures
with the next employer. Review wages, deductions, housing, and support arrangements carefully before
signing.
