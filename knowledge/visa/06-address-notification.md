# Address Notification (住居地届出)

## The 14-Day Rule
Whenever you move, you must notify your new address **within 14 days** of moving in. For residence
card holders this is done at the **municipal office (区役所・市役所)**, which records it on the
resident register (住民票) and on the back of your residence card, and forwards the information to
the Immigration Services Agency. You do not file a separate address notice at immigration when you
go through the municipal office.

## First Address After Arriving From Abroad
New residents arriving from overseas register their first address with the municipal office within
14 days of settling in. Bring your residence card (or passport with the "residence card to be
issued later" stamp) and your passport.

## Moving Within Japan
- Moving to a **different municipality**: file a moving-out notification (転出届) at your old
  municipality, then a moving-in notification (転入届) at the new one within 14 days.
- Moving **within the same municipality**: file a moving notification (転居届) within 14 days.

## Why It Matters
A correct registered address is the basis for:
- National Health Insurance (国民健康保険) and National Pension (国民年金)
- Resident tax (住民税)
- Receiving your My Number notification and official mail
- Visa renewal and permanent residency reviews, which check that you reported addresses on time

## Common Mistakes
- Forgetting the moving-out notification when leaving a municipality.
- Missing the 14-day window, which can be viewed unfavorably in future immigration applications.
