# Remote Work for Foreign Employers

Remote work while physically in Japan can raise status-of-residence questions even when the employer
is overseas. This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

## Status Fit

Japanese residence statuses are based on the activity conducted in Japan. Many work statuses assume a
Japanese accepting organization, contract, or business basis. A foreign remote employer may not fit
unless the status category and documents support the arrangement.

## Common Scenarios

A permanent resident or spouse-status holder may have broader work flexibility. A work-status holder,
student, dependent, or short-term visitor has tighter limits. Freelance or side remote work may need
permission or a different status.

## Documents to Review

- Current residence status and any permission conditions on the residence card.
- Employment or service contract, employer location, duties, hours, and payment flow.
- Tax, social-insurance, and business-registration documents if the work is ongoing.

## Cautions

Do not assume that online work is outside immigration rules because the client is abroad. Confirm the
status fit before relying on foreign remote income to live in Japan or renew a status.
