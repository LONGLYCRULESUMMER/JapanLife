# Dependent Visa (家族滞在)

## Who Qualifies
The Dependent status (家族滞在) is for the **spouse and children** of a person holding most work
or student statuses. The sponsoring resident must show they can financially support the dependent
(income and savings evidence).

## Work Restrictions
A Dependent may work only after obtaining **Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That
Permitted (資格外活動許可)**, and then only up to **28 hours per week**. A dependent who wants to
work full-time must change to an appropriate work status.

## Applying From Abroad vs Inside Japan
- **From abroad**: the sponsor files a Certificate of Eligibility (在留資格認定証明書) application
  in Japan; the dependent then applies for a visa at a Japanese embassy or consulate.
- **Inside Japan** (e.g. a child born in Japan, or a spouse already here on another status): file a
  change or acquisition of status application at the Immigration Services Agency.

## Typical Documents
- Application form and ID photo
- Passport and residence card (if already in Japan)
- Proof of the relationship: marriage certificate or birth certificate
- The sponsor's certificate of employment and income / tax documents

## Dependent vs Spouse of a Japanese National
"Dependent (家族滞在)" is for family of a **foreign** resident. If your spouse is a **Japanese
national or permanent resident**, the correct status is **Spouse of a Japanese National (日本人の
配偶者等)** or Spouse of a Permanent Resident, which has fewer work restrictions.
