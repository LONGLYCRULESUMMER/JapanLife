# Convenience-Store Certificates (コンビニ交付)

Many municipalities let residents print certificates at convenience-store kiosks using a My Number Card.
Availability depends on the municipality and certificate type.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents who need common municipal certificates such as a residence certificate, seal
registration certificate, tax certificate, or family-register-related certificate where supported.

## Where to Handle It

Use the multifunction copy machine at participating convenience stores. Your municipality must support
the service, and the certificate must be available for your registered address or registered domicile.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- My Number Card with a valid user certificate.
- Four-digit PIN for certificate issuance.
- Certificate type, number of copies, and fee.
- Details such as whether My Number, household members, or tax year should be shown.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Service hours are longer than ward office hours but may exclude maintenance periods, year-end closures,
or municipal system downtime. Check the printed certificate immediately for the requested details.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Name display, aliases, household details, and certificate availability can differ by municipality. If a
visa or employer request specifies exact wording, confirm the required certificate details before printing.
