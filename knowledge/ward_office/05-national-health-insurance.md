# National Health Insurance (国民健康保険)

## Who Enrolls
Japan has universal health coverage. If you are **not** covered by an employer's health insurance
(社会保険 / 健康保険), you must enroll in **National Health Insurance (国民健康保険, NHI)** at your
ward or city office. This includes students, the self-employed, freelancers, and many part-time
workers. Enrollment is mandatory for residents staying 3 months or more.

## When to Enroll
Enroll within **14 days** of:
- Moving to Japan or to a new municipality, or
- Leaving an employer's health insurance (e.g. quitting a job).

Coverage is backdated to the date you became eligible, so delaying does not reduce what you owe.

## What It Covers
- **70%** of most medical and dental costs (you pay the remaining **30%**).
- A **High-Cost Medical Care** system (高額療養費) caps your monthly out-of-pocket costs based on
  income.

## Premiums
NHI premiums are set by each municipality and are based on your **previous year's income** and the
number of insured people in your household. They are billed separately from employer insurance and
paid by payment slips or bank transfer. Low-income households can apply for reductions.

## When You Leave or Change Jobs
- Starting a job with 社会保険: leave NHI at the ward office and avoid paying twice.
- Leaving Japan or moving: settle your NHI at the ward office and return your insurance card.
