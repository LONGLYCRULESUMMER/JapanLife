# Disability Certificate Overview (障害者手帳)

Disability certificates can connect residents with welfare services, discounts, and support programs.
This is general information; certification standards and benefits vary.

## Who This Applies To

This may apply to residents with physical, intellectual, or mental disabilities who want a municipal or
prefectural certificate. The certificate type depends on the condition and assessment process.

## Where to Handle It

Start at the disability welfare section of your ward or city office. The office explains the certificate
type, required medical forms, photo rules, and submission route.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Identification, residence information, and My Number details.
- Application form and photo.
- Doctor's certificate, diagnosis form, or specialist documents requested for the certificate type.
- Existing medical, welfare, or pension documents if relevant.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Processing can take weeks or longer because medical review may be needed. After approval, ask how renewal,
updates, address changes, and related service applications are handled.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Foreign residents can ask the welfare counter about eligibility, language support, and how residence
status or length of stay affects specific benefits. Bring an interpreter or request support if medical
terms are difficult.
