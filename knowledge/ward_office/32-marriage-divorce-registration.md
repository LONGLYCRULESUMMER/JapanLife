# Marriage and Divorce Registration (婚姻届・離婚届)

Marriage and divorce notifications are family-status procedures filed with municipalities. This page is
general information; requirements can depend on nationality and personal circumstances.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to couples registering a marriage or divorce in Japan, including cases where one or both
partners are foreign nationals.

## Where to Handle It

Submit the notification at the family registry or resident affairs counter of a ward, city, town, or
village office. The office can explain local form checks and accepted supporting documents.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Marriage or divorce notification form.
- Passports, residence cards, and identification.
- Certificate of legal capacity to marry or other home-country documents when required.
- Japanese translations of foreign-language documents.

## Timing and Follow-Up

A marriage or divorce is generally recorded when the notification is accepted. Afterward, check whether
residence records, health insurance, pension, taxes, childcare, or immigration-related information needs
updating.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Requirements can differ by nationality and by the foreign documents available. Ask your embassy or
consulate about home-country documents and whether the event must also be reported outside Japan.
