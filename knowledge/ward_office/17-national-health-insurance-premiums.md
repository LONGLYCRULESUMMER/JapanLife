# National Health Insurance Premium Basics (国民健康保険料)

National Health Insurance premiums are municipal charges for residents enrolled in NHI. Use this as
general information and confirm exact billing with your ward or city office.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to people enrolled in **National Health Insurance (国民健康保険)**, such as self-employed
workers, students, freelancers, unemployed residents, and others not covered by employer health insurance.

## Where to Handle It

The NHI section of your ward or city office handles enrollment, billing, payment slips, bank transfer
setup, and questions about household premium amounts.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card and My Number information.
- NHI card or eligibility documents.
- Income information, if requested, especially after moving from overseas or another municipality.
- Payment notices or premium slips when asking about a bill.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Premiums are usually billed in installments during the fiscal year. Payment due dates are printed on the
notice. NHI enrollment and withdrawal changes are commonly reported within **14 days** of the event.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Premiums are often based on previous-year income and household composition. If you recently arrived in
Japan or had income overseas, ask the municipality how it confirms income for billing purposes.
