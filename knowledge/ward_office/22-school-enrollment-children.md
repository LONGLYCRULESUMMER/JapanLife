# School Enrollment for Children (就学)

Municipalities and boards of education handle enrollment in local public elementary and junior high
schools. This page is general information for families planning school procedures.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to families with school-age children who move into a municipality, arrive from overseas, or
need to transfer schools. Public elementary and junior high schools are assigned mainly by address.

## Where to Handle It

Contact the municipal board of education, school affairs section, or ward/city office education counter.
The assigned school may also ask you to visit after the enrollment decision.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Child and guardian residence cards or identification.
- Resident record or address confirmation.
- Previous school records, transcripts, or enrollment certificates if available.
- Vaccination, health, or language support information requested by the school.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Japan's school year starts in April, but transfer procedures are possible after moving. Contact the board
of education soon after address registration so school assignment and classroom preparation can proceed.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Foreign children can consult the municipality about public school enrollment and Japanese-language
support. Ask about international classrooms, interpreters, school lunch, costs, and required supplies.
