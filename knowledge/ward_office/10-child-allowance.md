# Child Allowance and Childbirth Support (児童手当・出産)

Japan provides cash support for raising children. These benefits are paid to **residents
regardless of nationality**, so foreign families who are registered residents can receive them.

## Child Allowance (児童手当)

Since the **October 2024** expansion:

- **No income limit** — all eligible households qualify.
- Paid until the **end of March of the fiscal year the child turns 18** (through high school).
- Monthly amounts:

| Child order | Under 3 | Age 3 to end of high school |
|---|---|---|
| 1st and 2nd child | ¥15,000 | ¥10,000 |
| 3rd child onward | ¥30,000 | ¥30,000 |

Payments are made **six times a year** (in even months), each covering the previous two months.
Apply at your ward/city office; allowances generally start the month after you apply, so apply
promptly.

## Childbirth Lump-Sum (出産育児一時金)

Health insurance pays a **lump-sum of about ¥500,000 per child** to help with delivery costs,
usually paid directly to the hospital under the direct-payment system.

## Notification of Birth (出生届)

Submit a **birth notification (出生届) within 14 days** of birth at the ward office. This registers
the child and enables health insurance, My Number, and child allowance.

## Maternal and Child Health Handbook (母子健康手帳)

When you become pregnant, get the **母子健康手帳** at the ward office. It records pregnancy
checkups, the birth, and the child's vaccinations and health checkups, and comes with checkup
vouchers.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Apply for child allowance the same day** you register the birth or move in — it is generally
   not back-paid far.
2. The childbirth lump-sum is usually paid **directly to the hospital**.
3. Keep the **母子手帳** with you for checkups and vaccinations.
