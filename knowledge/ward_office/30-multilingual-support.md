# Multilingual Support (多言語支援)

Local governments and international associations provide multilingual information to help residents use
public services. Availability differs by municipality and language.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents who prefer information in a language other than Japanese or need help
understanding municipal notices, disaster information, school documents, or healthcare guidance.

## Where to Handle It

Check your ward/city office, local international association, municipal website, community center, or
multicultural coexistence portal. Some areas offer interpretation by phone or tablet at counters.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- The notice, form, or topic you need help with.
- Residence card or account number if the consultation concerns a specific procedure.
- Preferred language, contact details, and deadline.
- Any previous translations or explanations you received.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Arrange support before appointments or application deadlines. Interpretation services may require advance
booking, and some languages may only be available on certain days.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Use simple Japanese terms or show the original document when asking for help. If translated material and
Japanese forms differ, the municipality may treat the Japanese form as the official version.
