# Public Housing Overview (公営住宅)

Public housing programs provide rental housing operated by municipalities, prefectures, or public housing
bodies. This page is general orientation and not a guarantee of eligibility.

## Who This Applies To

This may apply to households seeking lower-cost rental housing and meeting income, family, residency, and
housing-need requirements set by the program.

## Where to Handle It

Check the housing section of your ward or city office, prefectural housing corporation, or local public
housing application center. Application windows and property lists are usually published locally.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Household identification and residence information.
- Income, tax, and employment documents.
- Current housing details, lease information, or proof of housing need.
- Family relationship documents when requested.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Applications often open during fixed periods and may use a lottery or priority screening. Missing a window
usually means waiting for the next recruitment period.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Programs may require valid residence status, local residency, income confirmation, and Japanese-language
forms. Ask whether translated guides, consultation appointments, or assistance from an international
association are available.
