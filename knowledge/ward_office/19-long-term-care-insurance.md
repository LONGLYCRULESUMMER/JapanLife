# Long-Term Care Insurance (介護保険)

Japan's long-term care insurance system supports residents who need certified care services. This page is
general orientation; municipalities decide procedures and service details.

## Who This Applies To

Residents generally join the long-term care insurance system from age **40**. People aged 65 or older can
apply for care-need certification, and people aged 40 to 64 may qualify for services for designated
age-related conditions.

## Where to Handle It

The long-term care insurance or elderly welfare section of your ward or city office handles premiums,
care-need certification applications, and care manager referrals.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Health insurance card and identification.
- My Number information, if requested.
- Doctor or hospital details related to the care need.
- Any municipal notices about long-term care premiums or certification.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Premiums and application timing depend on age and situation. Apply for care-need certification when care
services are needed; the municipality reviews the application and sends a certification result.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Foreign residents who are registered residents may be included in the system. Ask about language support,
family interpretation rules, and whether translated explanations of care services are available.
