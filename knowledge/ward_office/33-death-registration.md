# Death Registration (死亡届)

A death notification is filed with a municipality after a death in Japan. This page is general orientation
for the municipal procedure and related follow-up.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to family members or other authorized reporters handling the death of a resident or a person
who died in Japan.

## Where to Handle It

File the death notification at the family registry or resident affairs counter of a ward, city, town, or
village office. Funeral homes often explain local filing and cremation permit steps.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Death notification form with the medical death certificate section completed.
- Reporter identification and contact details.
- Information about the deceased, such as address, nationality, and date of birth.
- Residence card, insurance card, pension documents, or municipal cards for later follow-up, if available.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Death notification is generally filed within **7 days after learning of the death**. After filing, ask the
municipality about health insurance, pension, tax, resident record, and benefit procedures.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

For a foreign national, the family may also need to contact the person's embassy or consulate and handle
residence card or immigration-related steps. Municipal staff can explain which local counters to visit.
