# Address Change Within the Same City (転居届)

Use this as general orientation when you move to a different home in the same municipality. Forms and
counter names vary by city, so confirm details with your local office.

## Who This Applies To

This procedure is for residents who move from one address to another **within the same city, ward, town,
or village**. It updates your resident record without creating a moving-out certificate.

## Where to Handle It

File a **moving-within-city notification (転居届)** at the resident registration counter of your ward or
city office. Some municipalities also let you update related services, such as National Health Insurance
and child allowance, during the same visit.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence cards for all foreign residents moving in the household.
- My Number Cards or notification documents, if you have them.
- National Health Insurance card, child medical subsidy card, or other municipal cards that show your
  address.
- New address, move date, and household member details.

## Timing and Follow-Up

The notification is generally filed within **14 days after moving**. After the address update, check
whether your My Number Card, insurance card, pension records, school records, and benefit notices also
need updated address information.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Your residence card address is updated through the municipal office procedure. Bring each family member's
card and ask whether everyone must appear in person or whether one household representative can file for
the group.
