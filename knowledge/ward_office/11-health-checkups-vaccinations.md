# Health Checkups and Vaccinations (健診・予防接種)

Your municipality offers subsidized health checkups and vaccinations across all ages. Notices are
mailed to your **registered address**, so keeping your address up to date matters.

## Infant and Child Checkups (乳幼児健診)

Free checkups are offered at set ages (for example, around 3–4 months, 1.5 years, and 3 years).
The ward office mails invitations and the checkups are recorded in the 母子健康手帳.

## Child Vaccinations (定期予防接種)

Routine childhood immunizations are **subsidized (often free)** when done on schedule. The schedule
and record are kept in the 母子手帳, and the ward provides vouchers/coupons.

## Adult Specific Health Checkup (特定健診)

For National Health Insurance members aged **40–74**, the **specific health checkup (特定健診)**
screens for metabolic-syndrome risk (blood pressure, blood sugar, lipids). It is low-cost or free.

## Cancer Screenings (がん検診)

Municipalities offer subsidized **cancer screenings** (e.g., stomach, lung, colon, breast,
cervical) for eligible ages. Notices and vouchers arrive by mail.

## How to Use Them

- Watch for **mailed notices and vouchers** sent to your registered address.
- Bring the voucher, your insurance card, and ID to a participating clinic.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Update your address** at the ward office so checkup and vaccination notices reach you.
2. Many checkups are **free or low-cost** — use them.
3. Keep the **母子手帳** updated for children's checkups and shots.
