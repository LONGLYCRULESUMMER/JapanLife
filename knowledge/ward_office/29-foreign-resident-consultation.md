# Local Consultation Counters for Foreign Residents (外国人相談)

Many municipalities offer consultation counters for foreign residents. They help explain local procedures
and connect residents with appropriate offices.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to foreign residents who have questions about daily life, municipal procedures, taxes,
insurance, childcare, disasters, housing, or where to ask about a problem.

## Where to Handle It

Look for a foreign resident consultation desk at the ward/city office, local international association,
multicultural center, or municipal call center. Some regions provide phone, email, or online consultation.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card or municipal notice related to the question.
- Letters, bills, application forms, or deadlines you want explained.
- Basic timeline of the issue and names of offices already contacted.
- Preferred language and contact method.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Contact the counter before the deadline on a notice or application. Consultation counters may have limited
hours or specific language days, so check the schedule early.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

These counters usually provide general guidance rather than acting as a legal representative. For legal,
immigration, tax, or medical decisions, ask which qualified office or professional can provide formal
advice.
