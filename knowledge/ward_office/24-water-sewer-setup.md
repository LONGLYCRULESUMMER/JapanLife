# Water and Sewer Setup (水道・下水道)

Water and sewer service is usually handled by a municipal or regional waterworks bureau. This page gives
general move-in and move-out orientation.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents starting, stopping, or changing water service for a home. Renters may handle it
directly or through a property manager depending on the building.

## Where to Handle It

Contact the local waterworks customer center, municipal water bureau, or designated online portal. In
Tokyo, water and sewer billing is handled through the metropolitan waterworks system.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Address, room number, and service start or stop date.
- Customer number or meter slip if available.
- Name, phone number, email, and payment method.
- Previous address or forwarding address for final bills.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Arrange service before moving in and request stopping service before moving out. Check the first bill,
payment method, and whether sewer charges are billed together with water charges.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Ask whether English or multilingual customer support is available. If your building has a management
company, confirm whether water is billed by the bureau, the landlord, or the building manager.
