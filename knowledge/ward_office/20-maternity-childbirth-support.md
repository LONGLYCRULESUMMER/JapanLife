# Maternity and Childbirth Support (母子保健)

Municipalities provide pregnancy, childbirth, and child health support through health centers and ward
or city offices. Use this as general information and confirm local programs.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to pregnant residents, parents expecting a child, and families with infants. Support can
include a maternal and child health handbook, prenatal checkup tickets, home visits, and child health
consultations.

## Where to Handle It

Notify pregnancy at the municipal health center, child health counter, or ward/city office. Many cities
issue the **Maternal and Child Health Handbook (母子健康手帳)** and explain local support programs there.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card or other identification.
- My Number information, if requested.
- Pregnancy confirmation details, expected delivery date, and clinic or hospital name.
- Bank account or insurance details for related benefit applications, if applicable.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Notify the municipality after pregnancy is confirmed so you can receive checkup coupons and guidance.
After birth, separate municipal procedures such as birth notification, child allowance, health insurance,
and infant health checks may have short deadlines.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Ask whether multilingual versions of the maternal handbook, interpreter support, or translated child
health materials are available. Bring notices from your clinic if you are unsure which counter to visit.
