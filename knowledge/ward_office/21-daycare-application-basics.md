# Daycare Application Basics (保育園申請)

Licensed daycare applications are usually managed by municipalities. This is general orientation; scoring,
forms, and deadlines vary widely by city.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to guardians seeking licensed nursery school or daycare for a child because of work, job
search, study, illness, childbirth, caregiving, or another recognized need for childcare.

## Where to Handle It

Apply through the childcare or nursery school section of your ward or city office. Some municipalities
allow online applications or consultation appointments before submission.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Application form and desired facility list.
- Proof of childcare need, such as employment certificates for guardians.
- Child and guardian identification, residence information, and My Number details.
- Tax or income documents, if the municipality cannot confirm them internally.

## Timing and Follow-Up

The largest intake is often for April admission, with deadlines months earlier. Monthly mid-year admission
may have separate cutoffs. Missing documents can delay screening, so check the city's application guide.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Eligibility is usually based on residency and childcare need, not nationality. Ask for translated facility
lists, interpretation, and how to submit overseas income or employment documents if relevant.
