# Child Medical Subsidy (子ども医療費助成)

Many municipalities subsidize children's out-of-pocket medical costs. This is general information;
coverage age, income rules, and copayments differ by municipality.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to households with children who are registered residents and enrolled in a health insurance
system. The eligible age range and household conditions depend on the local program.

## Where to Handle It

Apply at the child welfare, medical subsidy, or child allowance section of your ward or city office. Some
cities issue a child medical certificate card to show at clinics and pharmacies.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Child's health insurance card or eligibility information.
- Child and guardian identification, residence cards, and My Number details.
- Bank account information if reimbursements are used.
- Income or tax information, if requested.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Apply after birth, moving in, or joining health insurance. Some municipalities limit retroactive coverage,
so check the application timing printed in the local guide.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Rules may differ when a child recently arrived from overseas or is waiting for insurance enrollment. Ask
how to handle romanized names, translated documents, and reimbursement forms for visits outside the city.
