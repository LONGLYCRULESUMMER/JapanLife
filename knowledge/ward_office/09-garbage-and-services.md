# Garbage Rules and Municipal Services (ごみ分別・行政サービス)

## Garbage Sorting (ごみ分別)
Each municipality sets its own rules, but household waste is generally separated into:
- **Burnable (燃えるゴミ / 可燃ごみ)** — food scraps, paper that cannot be recycled
- **Non-burnable (燃えないゴミ / 不燃ごみ)** — small metal, glass, ceramics
- **Recyclables (資源ごみ)** — PET bottles, cans, glass bottles, clean paper, cardboard
- **Oversized waste (粗大ゴミ)** — furniture and large items, by paid appointment

Use the **designated bags (指定ごみ袋)** if your city requires them, and put waste out **by the
morning collection time** on the correct day, not the night before in many areas. Calendars are
available in multiple languages on city websites and apps.

## Oversized Waste (粗大ゴミ)
Items over a certain size cannot go in normal collection. Book a pickup with the oversized-waste
center, buy a disposal sticker (粗大ゴミ処理券) at a convenience store, attach it, and put the item
out on the assigned day.

## Childcare and Family Services (子育て支援)
Ward offices administer family support, including:
- **Child allowance (児童手当)** — apply after the birth or after moving in
- **Maternal and Child Health Handbook (母子健康手帳)** — issued during pregnancy
- **Nursery / daycare (保育園) and kindergarten (幼稚園)** applications
- Child medical-fee subsidies (子ども医療費助成)

## Other Municipal Services
- Multilingual consultation desks for residents
- Notification of vaccinations and health checkups
- Disaster-prevention (防災) information and hazard maps
- Issuance of various certificates (residence, tax, seal)
