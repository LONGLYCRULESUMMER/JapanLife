# Birth Notification (出生届)

When a child is born in Japan, a birth notification is filed with a municipality. This page is general
information about the municipal step and common related procedures.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to parents or authorized reporters of a child born in Japan, including children of foreign
national parents.

## Where to Handle It

File the birth notification at the family registry or resident affairs counter of a ward, city, town, or
village office. Many families also visit child allowance, health insurance, and child health counters.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Birth notification form with the doctor's or midwife's birth certificate section.
- Maternal and Child Health Handbook.
- Parent identification, residence cards, and passports.
- Health insurance and bank account details for related child procedures, if applicable.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Birth notification is generally filed within **14 days of birth**. Related steps may include resident
record creation, child allowance, health insurance enrollment, child medical subsidy, and infant health
checks.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

A child who does not automatically receive Japanese nationality may need nationality and immigration
procedures through the relevant embassy or immigration office. Ask the municipality which certificates are
available after filing.
