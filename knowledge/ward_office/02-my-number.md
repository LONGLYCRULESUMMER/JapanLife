# My Number (マイナンバー)

My Number is a 12-digit individual number assigned to every resident of Japan, used across tax,
social security, and disaster response. Foreign residents receive one after registering their
address.

## What Is My Number

A **12-digit ID (個人番号)** linked to you for life. Employers, banks, and the tax office use it to
match records. You receive a number after you register at the ward office.

## Notification vs Card

- **Notification** — historically a paper slip telling you your number.
- **My Number Card (マイナンバーカード)** — a plastic IC card with your photo that serves as
  official ID and unlocks online services. The card is optional but very useful.

## What the Card Does

- Acts as a **government-issued photo ID**.
- Lets you **print certificates** (住民票, 印鑑登録証明書, tax certificates) at convenience-store
  kiosks.
- Enables **e-Tax** login for online tax filing.
- Can function as your **health insurance card (マイナ保険証)** when linked.

## How to Get the Card

Apply **online, by mail, or at the ward office**, then pick up the card in person once it is ready
(this can take several weeks). You set a PIN for online use.

## When You Need My Number

- Starting a job (your employer collects it).
- Opening certain bank/securities accounts.
- Filing taxes and receiving some benefits.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. Keep your number **private** — share it only with employers, banks, and government as required.
2. The **My Number Card** greatly simplifies paperwork (conbini certificates, e-Tax, insurance).
3. **Update the card's address** at the ward office whenever you move.
