# Converting a Foreign License (外免切替)

If you hold an overseas driver's license, you can exchange it for a Japanese license through a
process known as **gaimen kirikae (外免切替)**, handled at a driver's licensing center
(運転免許試験場), not the ward office.

## What It Is

Rather than taking the full Japanese license course, qualifying foreign-license holders convert
their existing license after a document check and tests.

## Requirements

- A **valid foreign driver's license**.
- Proof you held the license and **drove for at least 3 months** in the issuing country after it
  was issued.
- An **official Japanese translation** of your license (e.g., from **JAF** or your embassy).
- **Passport, residence card, and 住民票** (and passport history showing your stay abroad).

## Process

1. Document screening and eligibility check.
2. A **knowledge test** and an **aptitude (eyesight) test**.
3. A **practical driving test** — though applicants from some countries/regions are **exempt** from
   the knowledge and practical tests under bilateral arrangements.

## International Driving Permit (国際運転免許)

An International Driving Permit lets you drive for up to **1 year** for short stays, but **residents
should convert** to a Japanese license.

## Tips for Foreigners

1. **Book early** — conversion test slots fill up at busy licensing centers.
2. Get the **JAF translation** of your license in advance.
3. Bring **passport history** proving the 3-month driving period abroad.
