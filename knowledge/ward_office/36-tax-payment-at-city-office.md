# Tax Payment at the City Office (市区町村での税金支払い)

Municipalities handle local taxes such as individual inhabitant tax. This page is general information
about paying city or ward tax notices.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents who receive municipal tax payment slips, resident tax notices, light vehicle tax
notices, or other local tax bills from a city, ward, town, or village.

## Where to Handle It

Pay using the methods printed on the notice, which may include banks, convenience stores, smartphone
payment, bank transfer, or the ward/city tax counter. The tax section handles questions about notices.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Tax notice and payment slips.
- Identification if visiting the counter.
- Bank account information for automatic transfer setup.
- Tax certificate request details if you need proof of payment or taxation.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Payment deadlines are printed on each slip. If you miss a deadline or cannot pay at once, contact the tax
section early to ask about available payment consultation procedures.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Resident tax certificates are often requested for housing, benefits, and immigration procedures. If moving
out of Japan, ask how final bills, overseas payment, forwarding, or a tax representative are handled.
