# Moving Out Abroad (国外転出)

Use this as general orientation before leaving Japan to live overseas. Local rules and linked procedures
vary, so confirm the steps with your municipality.

## Who This Applies To

This applies when a registered resident plans to leave Japan and end their address registration in the
municipality. It is different from moving to another city inside Japan.

## Where to Handle It

Submit a **moving-out notification for overseas relocation (国外転出届)** at the resident registration
counter of your ward or city office. You may also need to visit tax, National Health Insurance, pension,
and child-related counters.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card and passport.
- My Number Card, if you have one.
- National Health Insurance card and other municipal cards.
- Planned departure date and overseas contact address, if requested.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Municipalities usually accept the notification shortly before departure. Ask about settling resident tax,
insurance premiums, pension matters, and whether a tax representative or forwarding address is needed.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Leaving Japan may also involve immigration, residence card, school, banking, lease, and utility steps.
The ward office handles the municipal resident record; other agencies or private providers may have
separate procedures.
