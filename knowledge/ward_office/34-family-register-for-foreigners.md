# Family Register and Residence Records for Foreigners (戸籍・住民票)

Foreign residents are usually recorded in Japan's resident record system, while Japanese family registers
follow separate rules. This page gives general orientation.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to foreign residents who need proof of address, household details, family relationship, or
civil-status events such as marriage, divorce, birth, or death in Japan.

## Where to Handle It

Resident records are handled at the municipal resident affairs counter. Family-register-related
notifications are handled at the family registry counter of a ward, city, town, or village office.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card, passport, and My Number information when requested.
- Foreign civil-status certificates, relationship documents, or embassy documents if needed.
- Japanese translations of foreign-language documents.
- Desired certificate type, such as residence certificate or acceptance certificate.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Deadlines depend on the event being reported, such as birth, death, marriage, divorce, or moving. Ask
which certificate can prove the event after the notification is accepted.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Foreign nationals generally do not have their own Japanese family register, but some events can be
recorded through notifications and certificates. Name spelling, aliases, and relationship terms may need
careful confirmation.
