# My Number Card Renewal (マイナンバーカード更新)

My Number Cards and their electronic certificates have expiration dates. This page is general orientation
for renewal and updates.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents whose My Number Card or electronic certificate is nearing expiration, whose
residence period changed, or whose card details need updating after a move or name change.

## Where to Handle It

Renewal is usually handled through municipal My Number Card counters, reservation systems, or mail/online
steps described in the renewal notice. Some steps must be completed at the ward or city office.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- My Number Card and renewal notice, if received.
- Residence card for foreign residents.
- Identification required by the municipality.
- Card passwords or PINs for electronic certificate procedures.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Check the expiration date on the card and electronic certificate. For foreign residents with a limited
period of stay, card validity may be tied to the residence period, so update it after residence status
extension procedures when required.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Bring your residence card and confirm that the card validity matches your current period of stay. If your
status extension is still pending, ask the My Number counter how local renewal timing is handled.
