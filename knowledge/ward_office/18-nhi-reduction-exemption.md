# NHI Reduction and Exemption (国民健康保険料の減免)

Municipalities may have systems that reduce National Health Insurance premiums for households in certain
situations. This is general information; eligibility and forms differ locally.

## Who This Applies To

This may apply to NHI households with low income, a sudden income drop, job loss, disaster damage, or
other circumstances recognized by the municipality.

## Where to Handle It

Ask at the National Health Insurance or premium section of your ward or city office. Some offices have a
separate consultation counter for payment difficulty.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Residence card, My Number information, and NHI card.
- Premium notice or payment slips.
- Income documents, unemployment documents, disaster certificates, or other proof requested by the city.
- Household member and employment details.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Contact the office as soon as you know payment will be difficult. Some reductions must be requested before
or near the relevant due dates, and unpaid premiums may still need a separate payment plan.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

If your income documents are from overseas or in another language, ask whether a translation or additional
explanation is needed. A consultation can clarify options without assuming that a reduction is automatic.
