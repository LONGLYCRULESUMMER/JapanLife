# Moving Out: Ward Office Procedures (転出届)

## Moving-Out Notification (転出届)
When you move to a **different municipality**, file a moving-out notification (転出届) at your
**current** ward or city office. You can usually file from about **14 days before** your move date
and should do it around the time you leave. In return you receive a **moving-out certificate
(転出証明書)**, which you take to your new municipality.

## What You Need
- Residence card (在留カード) or other ID
- Personal seal (印鑑) if you registered one
- My Number card or notification, if you have it

## At Your New Address
Within **14 days** of moving in, file a moving-in notification (転入届) at the new ward office and
present the moving-out certificate. (Some municipalities now support an online moving-out step via
the My Number Card and the Mynaportal "引越しワンストップ" service.)

## Procedures That Follow You
Filing 転出 / 転入 also moves or updates:
- National Health Insurance (国民健康保険) — settle and re-enroll
- National Pension (国民年金) records
- Child allowance (児童手当) and school enrollment
- Seal registration (印鑑登録), which is cancelled when you leave and must be redone

## Moving Within the Same Municipality
If you stay in the same municipality, you do **not** file 転出 / 転入; instead file a moving
notification (転居届) within 14 days.
