# Bicycle Registration and Parking (防犯登録・駐輪場)

Bicycles are common in Japan, but registration and parking rules are local. Use this as general guidance
and check your municipality or bicycle shop for details.

## Who This Applies To

This applies to residents who buy, receive, transfer, or regularly park a bicycle. It also matters when
using station bicycle lots or municipal parking areas.

## Where to Handle It

Crime-prevention bicycle registration is usually handled at bicycle shops. Municipal bicycle parking
permits are handled by station parking offices, ward/city offices, or designated operators.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Identification such as a residence card.
- Purchase receipt, warranty card, transfer document, or previous registration details.
- Bicycle details such as frame number, color, and model.
- Address and contact information for parking applications.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Register a bicycle when buying it and update or transfer registration when ownership changes. Apply for
commuter parking before regular use near stations, because unauthorized parking may lead to removal fees.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

If buying a used bicycle, confirm that the previous owner canceled or transferred registration. Keep the
registration paperwork; it can help if police check ownership or if the bicycle is recovered after theft.
