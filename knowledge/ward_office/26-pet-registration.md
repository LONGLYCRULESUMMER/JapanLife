# Pet Registration (ペット登録)

Pet-related procedures are managed by municipalities and public health centers. This page focuses on
common municipal steps and general owner responsibilities.

## Who This Applies To

Dog owners generally need municipal dog registration and annual rabies vaccination procedures. Other pets
may be subject to local rules, housing rules, or animal welfare requirements.

## Where to Handle It

Contact the environmental health section, public health center, or ward/city office. Veterinary clinics
often help with rabies vaccination certificates and local registration paperwork.

## Documents and Details to Prepare

- Owner identification and address.
- Dog details such as name, breed, sex, color, birth date, and microchip information if available.
- Rabies vaccination certificate.
- Previous registration tag or transfer information when moving or changing owners.

## Timing and Follow-Up

Dog registration is generally done once, and rabies vaccination is handled annually. When moving, ask the
new municipality how to update the registered address and whether a new tag is issued.

## Notes for Foreign Residents

Check rental contracts and building rules before keeping a pet. If documents are in another language, ask
whether the municipality or veterinarian needs a translation of vaccination or microchip records.
